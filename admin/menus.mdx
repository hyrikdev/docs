Menus are lists of links. You edit them in **Online Store → Navigation**. Three menus are used on the site:

| Menu | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| **Main menu** | The header menu on desktop and the drawer menu on phones. |
| **Locations** | The list of cities on the locations overview (/pages/locations). |
| **Helpful Links** | The "Helpful links" column in the footer. |

## Main menu

The main menu has four top items: **3D Scanning**, **Reverse Engineering**, **Engineering Support** and
**Buy a Scanner**. The first three open a dropdown with their service pages.

- **Home** and **Contact Us** are left out on purpose: the logo goes home, and the header has its own
  **Contact us** button.
- Keep the menu short. With more items, the header runs out of room on laptop screens and starts hiding items
  under **More**.
- Menu item names keep their current capitals (for example "Reverse Engineering").

## Locations menu

Each item is a city or area, linked to its location page. Some cities have a second page nested under them,
shown as a small link below the city name on the locations overview.

When you add a new location page, add it here so visitors can find it. See
[Add a location page](/how-to/add-location-page).

## Edit a menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Go to **Online Store → Navigation** and click the menu's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change items">
    - To add an item, click **Add menu item**, type the **Label**, then search for the page in **Link** and
      pick it.
    - To rename an item, click it and change the label.
    - To reorder, drag an item by its handle. Drag it under another item and to the right to nest it.
    - To remove an item, click it and choose **Delete**. This only removes the link, not the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save menu**. The change is live straight away on every theme that uses the menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Menus are shared by all themes. A change you make while preparing a theme copy shows on the live site too.
</Warning>
