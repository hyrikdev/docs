This guide is for the Tangent Solutions team members who update the website, usetangent.com.
You don't need to write code to follow it. If you used the old WordPress site, start with
[Coming from WordPress](/from-wordpress/overview).

Developers who maintain the theme have their own section: the **Technical** tab at the top of this guide.

## What the site is built on

- **Shopify** runs the site. You sign in to the Shopify admin to change pages, blog posts, menus and images.
- **Horizon** is the Shopify theme. A theme is the design of the site: its layout, fonts and colors.
- **Tangent blocks** were added to the theme for this site. They give you the ruled grid, the blue headings,
  the line icons and the other parts of the Tangent design.

You edit most of the site in the **theme editor**. It shows the page as visitors see it, with a list of its
parts on the left. You click a part, change it, and save.

## How this guide is organized

1. **Coming from WordPress**: what maps to what, and where each plugin's job went.
2. **Getting started**: the theme editor, previewing and publishing, sections and blocks, undoing changes.
3. **Shopify and Horizon**: the theme, its settings, and how a page is put together.
4. **Editing pages**: how the pages are set up and how to edit them.
5. **Page recipes**: each page type, section by section.
6. **Building blocks**: one page per part of the site, with its settings in plain words.
7. **Content in the Shopify admin**: what you change outside the theme editor.
8. **Design rules**: simple do's and don'ts that keep the design consistent.
9. **Common tasks**: short recipes, such as changing the phone number or adding a blog post.
10. **Help**: what to do when a change doesn't show up.
