title: "Editing content"
description: "Change text, replace images, reorder and hide blocks."
---

All of these happen in the theme editor. Open the page first: see
[Open a page in the theme editor](/editing-pages/how-pages-work#open-a-page-in-the-theme-editor).

## Change text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the text">
    Click the text in the preview, or find its **Text** block in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it in the text box">
    The text box has simple buttons for bold, italic, links and lists. Keep headings as headings: the
    heading level is set in the box (for example **Heading 2**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the style alone">
    Under **Tangent style**, the text already has the right style, for example **Eyebrow** or
    **Heading large**. You don't need to change it to edit the words. See [Text styles](/blocks/text-styles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Check the desktop and the mobile view, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
For cards, the text is in the card itself: click the card and edit its **Title**, **Text** and **Label**.
See [Card list](/blocks/card-list).
</Note>

### Writing tips

- Use sentence case: only the first word and names get a capital letter. "Reverse engineering services",
  not "Reverse Engineering Services".
- Keep titles short. Long titles wrap onto many lines on phones.
- Don't type the phone number into plain text. Use a call button: see [Buttons](/blocks/buttons).

## Replace an image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the image">
    Click the image in the preview, or its **Image** block (or the **Card** it belongs to) in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a new image">
    Under **Image**, click **Change**. Pick an image you already uploaded, or click **Upload** to add one
    from your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add alt text">
    Alt text describes the image for people who can't see it and for search engines. Describe what the photo
    shows in a short sentence, for example "Technician scanning a car wheel with a handheld scanner". Add it
    in **Content → Files**: see [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the crop">
    Photos are cropped to fit their frame. If the important part is cut off, change
    **Crop position, vertical**. See [Images](/blocks/images).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use photos at least 1600 pixels wide for wide frames, and keep files under about 1 MB so pages load fast.
</Tip>

## Reorder blocks or sections

In the left sidebar, hover a section or block. A handle with six dots appears on its left. Drag the line up or
down. You can only drop a block inside the same kind of place it came from, for example a card among other
cards.

## Hide a block

1. Hover the block or section in the left sidebar.
2. Click the eye icon. The block disappears from the page but keeps its content.
3. Click the eye again to show it.
4. Click **Save**.

## Add a block

1. In the left sidebar, find the section or group you want to add to.
2. Click **Add block** at the bottom of its list.
3. Pick the block, for example **Text**, **Button** or **Card**.
4. Set its style to match the blocks around it. The easiest way: copy the settings of a block next to it.

<Tip>
To add a card that looks exactly like the others, open the menu of an existing card in the sidebar (the **…**
button next to it, or right-click it) and choose **Duplicate**, then edit the copy. This keeps the icon size, style and spacing the same.
</Tip>

## Remove a block

Click the block, then **Remove block** at the bottom of its settings. Prefer hiding when you're not sure:
see [Undo and versions](/getting-started/undo-and-versions).
