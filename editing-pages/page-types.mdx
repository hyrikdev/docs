## Homepage

The homepage (template **Home page**) is a stack of sections: **Hero**, **Logo grid**, **Services**,
**Process**, **Stats**, **Why Tangent**, **Capabilities**, **Scanning options** and **Call to action**.
Everything on it is edited in the theme editor.

<Note>
The homepage icons are the reference for the whole site. When a page talks about the same idea, it uses the
same icon. See [Icons](/design-rules/icons).
</Note>

## Service pages

There are 13 service pages, such as 3D scanning services, reverse engineering and CAD design services. Each
has its own template (**service-** + address).

<Frame caption="The top of a service page">
  <img src="/images/service-page.png" alt="The 3D scanning services page with breadcrumbs, the page title, two buttons and a photo" />
</Frame>

A service page usually has:

- a hero with breadcrumbs (Home > Services > page), the title, an intro, buttons and a photo;
- option cards, industry cards, file formats, a gallery, the process steps and "why us" cards;
- a FAQ list (see [FAQ](/blocks/faq));
- a closing section with the quote form.

The sections differ from page to page. Edit everything in the theme editor, except the FAQ questions, which
live in the Shopify admin.

## Location pages

There are 46 location pages, one per city or area, such as Chicago, Phoenix or Orange County. Each has its
own template (**location-** + address).

<Frame caption="The top of a location page">
  <img src="/images/location-page.png" alt="The Chicago location page with breadcrumbs Home / Locations / 3D scanning Chicago, the title and a photo" />
</Frame>

A location page usually has: the hero with breadcrumbs (Home > Locations > page), an intro with four
"at a glance" cards, a photo gallery, a CAD row, the list of services, the industries, client quotes, recent
projects and the quote form at the end.

To list a new location on the locations overview, see [Add a location page](/how-to/add-location-page).

## Landing pages

Landing pages are made for ads and search campaigns.

- **Long landing pages**, such as 3D scanning services for CNC or Buy a 3D scanner, look like service pages.
- **Short landing pages**, such as Reverse engineering company or Free estimates, have a hero with the copy on
  the left and a form on the right, then a few sections below. Some of their texts come from the page's own
  fields in the admin. See [Text that comes from the page's own fields](/editing-pages/how-pages-work#text-that-comes-from-the-pages-own-fields).

## Services overview

The services overview (/pages/services, template **services**) shows an intro and one card per service.
The cards are blocks in the **Services we provide** section. Edit, add or reorder them there.

## Locations overview

The locations overview (/pages/locations, template **locations-hub**) lists every location page in ruled
columns. That list is the **Locations** menu. To add, rename or remove a city, edit the menu in the admin:
see [Menus](/admin/menus).

  <img src="/images/locations-hub.png" alt="The locations overview with city names in ruled columns" />

## Blog and articles

- The blog list (/blogs/news, template **blog**) shows the title "Blog", tag filter chips and the post cards,
  with a **Load more** button.
- The chips are built from the tags of the posts. Adding a tag to a post adds a chip.
- Each article (template **article**) shows its title, picture and text, with a column on the right that holds
  the call buttons and the recent articles.

## Product page

The HandySCAN Black Elite scanner has a product page (template **quote**). The site doesn't sell online, so
the page has no cart: visitors call or ask for a quote instead.

- The top part (photos, title, description) comes from the product in the Shopify admin (**Products**).
- The sections below are blocks in the theme editor: applications, photos, benefits, specs, software,
  portability and the call to action. Their texts, including **Specs**, **Software** and **Completely
  portable**, are typed in the theme editor. See [Product page](/page-recipes/product-page).
