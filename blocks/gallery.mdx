The site has two kinds of photo grids:

- **Card list** with the **Gallery** layout: used on service and location pages. Each card is one photo with an
  optional caption. See [Card list](/blocks/card-list).
- **Project gallery**: used on the portfolio page. Each photo is a **Project** block, and a click opens the photo
  full screen (the "lightbox").

### Gallery settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Layout** | **Ruled tiles** puts the photos on the site's 1px lines, with the caption over the photo on hover. **Cards** is the theme's plain look. The portfolio uses **Ruled tiles**. |
| **Columns** | **Ruled tiles** only: photos per row on desktop. |
| **Open photos in a lightbox** | A click opens the photo full screen with its title, the count, and previous, next and close buttons. |
| **Columns on mobile** | 1 or 2. |
| **Image aspect ratio** | The shape of each tile. |

### Project settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Image** | The photo. |
| **Caption** | The project name, shown on the tile. Also used as the alt text when the image has none. |
| **Tag** | A short label, for example In-house, Shop or Mobile. |
| **Link** | Optional, for example a case study article. |

## The lightbox

With **Open photos in a lightbox** on, visitors can click any photo to see it whole. They can move with the
arrows (or the arrow keys) and close it with the **×** button, the Escape key or a click outside the photo.
The title under the photo is the image's alt text, or else the caption.

## Add a photo to the portfolio

1. Open the **portfolio** template in the theme editor.
2. In the **Gallery** section, click the **Project gallery** block, then **Add block** → **Project**.
3. Pick the **Image** and type the **Caption**.
4. Drag the new project to where it should appear.
5. Click **Save**.
