## What it does

The site has two kinds of photo grids:

- **Card list** with the **Gallery** layout: used on service and location pages. Each card is one photo with an
  optional caption. See [Card list](/blocks/card-list).
- **Project gallery**: used once, on the portfolio page. Each photo is a **Project** block (12 on the
  portfolio), and a click opens the photo full screen (the "lightbox").

This page is about the **Project gallery**. On the portfolio it's a ruled grid of square photos, four per row on
large screens and two on tablets and phones. The project name shows over the photo on hover.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the **portfolio** template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    In the sidebar, open the **Gallery** section and select **Project gallery**. The photos are the **Project**
    blocks inside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add a photo, select **Project gallery** and choose **Add block › Project**.

## The lightbox

With **Open photos in a lightbox** on, visitors can click any photo to see it whole. They can move with the
arrows (or the arrow keys) and close it with the **×** button, the Escape key or a click outside the photo.
The title under the photo is the image's alt text, or else the caption.

## Add a photo to the portfolio

1. Open the **portfolio** template in the theme editor.
2. In the **Gallery** section, click the **Project gallery** block, then **Add block › Project**.
3. Pick the **Image** and type the **Caption**.
4. Drag the new project to where it should appear.
5. Click **Save**.

## Settings

Keep the portfolio's **Ruled tiles** layout: with it, **Columns** sets the photos per row and the
**Minimum tile width** and **Corner radius** settings don't apply. For each photo, the **Caption** matters most:
it's the name on the tile and in the lightbox.

### Project gallery

{/* settings:begin source=block:ts-gallery level=4 */}

Used once across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Layout** | Cards, Ruled tiles | Cards | Ruled tiles | Ruled tiles: the tiles on the site's 1px grid lines, the caption over the photo on hover (always shown at the bottom on touch screens). |
| **Columns** | 2–6 | 4 | 4 | Shown when **Layout** is Ruled tiles. Photos per row on screens 990px and wider. |
| **Open photos in a lightbox** | On, Off | Off | On | Each photo opens whole in a dialog with its title (the image alt, else the caption), the count, previous, next and close. |
| **Minimum tile width** | 180–400px | 250px | 250px | Cards only: on screens 750px and wider, columns are added while each tile is at least this wide. Ruled tiles use Columns instead. |
| **Columns on mobile** | 1, 2 | 2 | 2 | Cards: below 750px. Ruled tiles: below 990px. |
| **Image aspect ratio** | 4:3, 3:2, Square, Portrait 4:5 | 4:3 | Square | — |
| **Corner radius** | 0–24px | 12px | 0px | Cards only. Ruled tiles always have square corners. |

{/* settings:end */}

### Project

{/* settings:begin source=block:_ts-project level=4 */}

Used 12 times across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Image** | Image | — | Set on all | — |
| **Caption** | Text | “3D scanning of an engine head” | Set on all | The project name, shown on the tile. Also used as the alt text when the image has none. |
| **Tag** | Text | “In-house” | Empty | A short label shown with the caption, for example In-house, Shop or Mobile. |
| **Link** | Link | — | Empty | Optional, e.g. a case study article. |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None. The photos are picked in each project's **Image** setting (see [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images)).

## Limitations

- The photo grids of service and location pages are **Card list** blocks, not project galleries. Their settings
  are on the [Card list](/blocks/card-list) page.
- Give each photo alt text in **Content → Files**: the lightbox shows it as the title, and falls back to the
  caption when it's empty.
