## What it does

Shows a series of steps beside a large photo. One step is active at a time: its text opens under its title and
its photo fills the image area. The steps move on by themselves, or visitors pick one. Each step is a
**Process step** block inside the process.

On large screens the photo takes two thirds of the width and the list of steps the last third. On phones the
photo and the list are stacked.

- Each step shows its number and title, for example "01. Scope". The active step is highlighted, with its text
  under the title and a thin progress bar under it while the steps play.
- A counter on the photo shows which step is shown, for example "01 / 05".

The site has one process, on the **homepage**, in the **Process** section. It has five steps: Scope, Scan,
Process, Rebuild and Deliver, numbered "01." to "05.". Its **Accessible name** is
"From physical part to CAD file in 5 steps".

### How the steps move

- With **Autoplay** on, the next step opens after the **Time per step**.
- Autoplay pauses while the mouse is over the block or the visitor is using the keyboard inside it, and while
  the page isn't visible.
- Once a visitor clicks or taps a step, autoplay stops for good: they're in control.
- Visitors who have asked their device for reduced motion get no autoplay.
- Visitors can also move between steps with the arrow keys.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the **Home page** template (the first one the editor opens).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    In the sidebar, open the **Process** section, then its group, and select **Process**. The steps are the
    **Process step** blocks inside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add a step, select the **Process** block and choose **Add block › Process step**, then drag it into place.

<Note>
In the theme editor, click a **Process step** in the sidebar: the preview shows that step and holds it there
while you edit it.
</Note>

## Settings

Most changes are to the steps: their **Title**, **Text** and **Image**. On the **Process** block itself,
**Autoplay** and **Time per step** set how the steps move.

### Process

{/* settings:begin source=block:ts-process level=4 */}

Used once across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Autoplay** | On, Off | On | On | — |
| **Time per step** | 3–12s | 6s | 6s | Shown when **Autoplay** is on. |
| **Aspect ratio** | Auto, Square, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 | Auto | Auto | Auto follows the height of the step list (at least 520px on desktop, 420px on tablet) and uses 4:3 on mobile. A ratio sets the minimum height of the image. |
| **Accessible name** | Text | — | Set on all | Read by screen readers to name the list of steps, for example the section heading. |

#### Padding

| **Bottom** | 0–100px | 0px | 0px | — |
| **Left** | 0–100px | 0px | 0px | — |

{/* settings:end */}

### Process step

{/* settings:begin source=block:_ts-process-step level=4 */}

Used 5 times across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Number** | Text | — | Set on all | Shown before the title, for example 01. Leave empty to number the steps automatically by their order. |
| **Title** | Text | — | Set on all | A step without a title isn't shown on the site. |
| **Text** | Text | — | Set on all | Opens under the title while the step is active. |
| **Image** | Image | — | Set on all | Shown while this step is active. Give every step a photo of the same shape, so the image area doesn't jump. |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None. The steps are typed in the blocks, and the photos are picked in each step's **Image** setting (see
[Files and images](/admin/files-and-images)).

## Limitations

- The section heading above the process is made of separate **Text** blocks, not part of the process block.
- If the other steps have a **Number**, give a new step a number too and renumber the ones after it.
