The **Process** block shows a series of steps beside a large photo. One step is active at a time: its text
opens under its title and its photo fills the image area. The steps move on by themselves, or visitors pick one.
Each step is a **Process step** block inside the process.

## What it looks like

On large screens the photo takes two thirds of the width and the list of steps the last third. On phones the
photo and the list are stacked.

- Each step shows its number and title, for example "01. Scope". The active step is highlighted, with its text
  under the title and a thin progress bar under it while the steps play.
- A counter on the photo shows which step is shown, for example "01 / 05".

How the steps move:

- With **Autoplay** on, the next step opens after the **Time per step**.
- Autoplay pauses while the mouse is over the block or the visitor is using the keyboard inside it, and while
  the page isn't visible.
- Once a visitor clicks or taps a step, autoplay stops for good: they're in control.
- Visitors who have asked their device for reduced motion get no autoplay.
- Visitors can also move between steps with the arrow keys.

<Note>
In the theme editor, click a **Process step** in the sidebar: the preview shows that step and holds it there
while you edit it.
</Note>

## Block settings

### Process

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Autoplay** | Moves to the next step by itself. |
| **Time per step** | How long each step stays open with autoplay, from 3 to 12 seconds. The homepage uses 6. |
| **Aspect ratio** | The shape of the photo area. **Auto** follows the height of the step list (and 4:3 on phones). The other choices (**Square**, **4:3**, **3:2**, **16:9**) set its minimum height. |
| **Accessible name** | Read by screen readers to name the list of steps. Usually the section heading. |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, **Right**) | Space around the block. |

### Process step

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Number** | Shown before the title, for example "01.". Leave it empty to number the steps by their order. |
| **Title** | The step's name. A step without a title isn't shown on the site. |
| **Text** | The text that opens under the title while the step is active. |
| **Image** | The photo shown while this step is active. |

## Where it's used on the site

On the **homepage**, in the **Process** section. It has five steps: Scope, Scan, Process, Rebuild and Deliver,
numbered "01." to "05.", with **Autoplay** on and 6 seconds per step. Its **Accessible name** is
"From physical part to CAD file in 5 steps".

## Notes

- The section heading above the process is made of separate **Text** blocks, not part of the process block.
- Give every step a photo of the same shape, so the image area doesn't jump between steps.
- To add a step: click the **Process** block, then **Add block** → **Process step**, and drag it into place. If the
  other steps have a **Number**, give the new one a number too and renumber the ones after it.
