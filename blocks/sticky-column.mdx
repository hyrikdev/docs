---
title: "Sticky column"
description: "A ruled column beside the main text that stays in view while the page scrolls."
---

The **Sticky column** block is a narrow column that sits beside the main content of a page. Whatever you put
in it (buttons, a short heading, a list of recent articles) stays in view while visitors scroll down the long
text next to it. It's a container: you add other blocks inside it, the same way you add blocks to a group.

## What it looks like

On a large screen (990 pixels wide and up), the column sits to the right of the content, with a thin line on
its left side from top to bottom. As the visitor scrolls, the blocks inside it stay just under the header. If
the column holds a quote form, it gets a light gray background.

On tablets and phones there's no room for a second column, so the column moves under the content (or above
it, or is hidden: see **Below 990px**).

<Note>
In the **Page** and **Blog post** sections, blocks can only be stacked one under the other. There, the sticky
column places itself beside the block just before it, for example the post's text. Keep it right after that
block.
</Note>

## Block settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sticky offset** | The space between the header and the column while it sticks. The header's height is added by itself. |
| **Width** | The width of the column on large screens, from 240 to 480 pixels. The site uses 360. |
| **Below 990px** | Where the column goes on tablets and phones: **After the content**, **Before the content** or **Hidden**. |
| **Hide the whole section when hidden** | Only with **Hidden**. For a section that holds nothing but this column: below 990 pixels the whole section, with its top line, is hidden too. |
| **Centred** | For a column that's alone in its row: no line on its side, and its blocks centred. Centre the row as well. |
| **Gap** | The space between the blocks inside the column. |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, **Right**) | The space inside the column. The site uses 48 pixels on every side. |

## Where it's used on the site

- **Every blog article** (template **article**): the column beside the post holds the **Call Now** and
  **Get a Quote** buttons and the **Recent posts** list titled "Recent articles". On phones it comes after the
  post. See [Blog blocks](/blocks/blog-blocks#recent-posts).
- **The shared landing template** (template **landing**, a starting point for new landing pages): a
  "Get started" column beside the page text, with two buttons. It's hidden on tablets and phones. See
  [Landing pages](/page-recipes/landing-page#the-shared-landing-layouts).
- **The CNC landing page** (3D scanning services for CNC): the closing "Get started" row is a sticky column on
  its own, **Centred**, and hidden with its section on tablets and phones.

## Notes

- The column only sticks on large screens (990 pixels and up). Check the page on a phone after a change.
- To change the buttons in the article column, open the **article** template: the change applies to every
  blog post at once.
