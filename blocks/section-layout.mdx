## What it does

The Tangent look is a technical drawing grid: thin vertical lines on both sides of the page (the "rails"), a
thin line between sections, and thin lines between the cells of a row or grid. These lines don't come from a
special block. They come from a few settings Tangent added to two of Horizon's building parts:

- **Section**: the outer part of every content row on a page. Its **Tangent frame** settings draw the rails, the
  line across the top and the faint dot pattern. The site has 671 of these sections, on 83 page layouts.
- **Group**: a block that holds other blocks (texts, images, buttons, other groups). Its **Tangent grid**
  settings draw the lines between the blocks inside it and set the number of columns. The site has 1,732 groups,
  on 89 page layouts and in the footer.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **service-3d-scanning-services**). See
    [Open a page in the theme editor](/editing-pages/how-pages-work#open-a-page-in-the-theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the section">
    In the sidebar, click the section's name (for example **What we do**). Its settings open; scroll down to
    **Tangent frame**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a group">
    Expand the section in the sidebar and click a **Group** inside it. Scroll down to **Tangent grid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add a group, select a section or another group and choose **Add block › Group**. To add a section, it's
safest to copy one from another page so its lines match (see the note below).

## The section frame

Three settings under **Tangent frame** draw the frame of a section:

| Setting | What it does | On this site |
|---|---|---|
| **Rails** | Thin vertical lines at the page width. **Rails with inner padding** adds space inside the lines; **Rails, no inner padding** leaves it to the blocks. | **Rails, no inner padding** on every section. |
| **Top border across the page** | A thin line across the top of the section. | On for almost every section, so sections are divided by a line. |
| **Background pattern** | Faint blue dots under the blocks, inside the rails. See the choices below. | On a few text-only sections: page heads and closing calls to action. |

The **Background pattern** choices:

| Choice | Where the dots sit | On this site |
|---|---|---|
| **Dot lattice** | Fading out from the top center, behind a centered heading. | 10 sections: the **Why Tangent** heads of the homepage and three service pages, and the closing section of six location pages. |
| **Dot lattice at the end** | Fading in from the top corner on the far side, clear of left-aligned text. Hidden on phones. | 20 sections: most **Call to action** sections at the foot of a page, and five page heads. |
| **Dot lattice from the bottom start** | From the bottom corner under a title, beside its text. Hidden on phones. | 5 statement rows on service pages. |
| **Lattice at the foot (bottom start, closing rows)** | A low band in the bottom corner of a closing row (text and buttons). Hidden below 990 pixels. | 7 closing rows on landing pages. |

The dots are decoration only: screen readers skip them.

## Lines between cells

A **Group** holds other blocks. Under **Tangent grid**, its **Lines** setting draws thin lines between the blocks
inside, in place of the gap:

| Lines | What it does | Groups on this site |
|---|---|---|
| **None** | No lines; the blocks keep their gap. | 1,006 |
| **Lines between blocks** | A line between each block, in a row or a column. | 283 |
| **Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet** | The same, and the blocks stack one under the other below 990 pixels. Used for "text beside photo" rows. | 352 |
| **No lines, stacked on tablet** | No lines, and the blocks stack below 990 pixels. Used for a title beside its text. | 27 |
| **Grid with lines** | The blocks become equal columns on a ruled grid. Set **Columns on desktop**, **Columns on tablet** and **Columns on mobile**. | 63 |
| **Ruled grid, compact cells on phones** | The same ruled grid, with smaller padding in each cell on phones. | 1 (the facts under the homepage hero) |
| **Cards** | The blocks become separate bordered cards, 16 pixels apart, in the columns you set. | Not used |

For the column settings, desktop means screens 990 pixels wide and up, tablet 750 to 989 pixels, and mobile
under 750 pixels. The most common grid is 4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet and 2 on phones.

The lines on the left and right edges of a grid are trimmed: the section's rails draw those.

<Note>
Two-column rows split 50 / 50, and the column lines of one section line up with the lines of the next. If you
add a section, copy one from the same page so its lines match.
</Note>

The faint pixel squares or part drawings on the edge of some groups are a separate block: see
[Pixel mark](/blocks/mark).

## Settings

On a section, the settings that matter are under **Tangent frame**: keep **Rails** on **Rails, no inner
padding** and **Top border across the page** on, as on the other sections. On a group, **Lines** and the three
column counts under **Tangent grid** shape the row; for one half of a two-column row, set **Width** to
**Custom** at 50%. Horizon's own background, border and corner settings stay at their defaults on this site.

## Data and metafields

None. These are layout settings only; nothing needs to exist in the admin first.

## Limitations

- When **Lines** draws lines or cards, the group's **Gap** is ignored. The cells sit on the lines instead.
- The **Page**, **404**, **Blog posts**, **Blog post** and **Product information** sections (the parts that
  show a page's own content, a blog or the scanner's product page) have their own **Rails** and **Top border
  across the page** settings with the same look. **Page** and **404** also have **Background pattern**.
- A new section added with **Add section** starts with **Rails** on **None** and no top border. Set them as
  above, or copy a section from another page instead.
- Keep the dot patterns rare, in empty space and never over text. See
  [Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont#layout).
- Leave **Background color**, **Background media** and **Borders** on their defaults. Colored bands, boxes and
  rounded corners aren't part of the Tangent design.
