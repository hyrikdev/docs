The **Flow chart** block shows a process as a small diagram: each step or result is a cell on the ruled grid,
and arrows on the lines between the cells show what leads to what. Each cell is a **Flow node** block inside
the flow chart. The words are real text, so search engines and screen readers read them in order.

## What it looks like

On a large screen (990 pixels and up) the chart has 4 equal columns and 2 rows, with thin lines between the
cells. Columns 1 and 2 run the full height; columns 3 and 4 can be split into a top row and a bottom row, so
one step can branch into two results.

- A **Process step** cell shows its title only.
- A **Deliverable** cell (a result, such as a file type) has a light gray background, a small blue square, its
  title and a line under it.
- The arrows are small round marks on the line to the right of a cell.

On tablets (750 to 989 pixels) the chart has 2 columns. On phones it's a single column in block order; the
branch cells are indented, with a blue line on their left edge.

## Block settings

### Flow chart

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show only when** and **Value** | For a template shared by several pages: connect **Show only when** to a field of the page, and the chart shows only on pages where that field equals **Value**. Leave **Value** blank to always show the chart. |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**) | Space above and below the chart. |

### Flow node

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The name of the step or result, for example "Mesh File". |
| **Line** | Shown under the title, for example what a file is used for. |
| **Type** | **Process step**, or **Deliverable (muted cell, accent square)** for a result. |
| **Position** | Where the cell sits on large screens: **Column 1, both rows**, **Column 2, both rows**, **Columns 3 and 4, top row**, **Column 3, bottom row**, **Column 4, bottom row**, or **Auto** (in block order). |
| **Arrows** | **None**, **One, to the next cell**, or **Two, to the top and bottom rows** (for a step that branches). |

<Tip>
Keep the nodes in reading order in the sidebar. Phones show them in that order, one under the other.
</Tip>

## Where it's used on the site

On the **reverse engineering** service page, in the **What we do** section. It shows how a part becomes files:

| Cell | Type | Position | Arrows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3D Laser Scan | Process step | Column 1, both rows | One |
| Mesh File | Process step | Column 2, both rows | Two |
| STL & OBJ File, "Conceptual projects, 3D printing, digital assets" | Deliverable | Columns 3 and 4, top row | None |
| Reverse Engineering | Process step | Column 3, bottom row | One |
| STEP, XT, IGES, "3D, Manufacturable CAD Files" | Deliverable | Column 4, bottom row | None |

## Notes

- The **Position** choices are made for this shape: two steps, then a split into a top result and a bottom step
  with its own result. To change the words, edit the nodes. For a different shape, ask your developer.
- Placed straight in a section with the **Rails, no inner padding** frame, the chart runs from rail to rail and
  its lines meet the page's column lines. See [Section layout](/blocks/section-layout).
