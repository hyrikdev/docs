title: "Pixel mark"
description: "The faint drawing on the edge of a section: the logo's pixel squares or a scanned part."
---

The **Pixel mark** block draws a faint decoration on the edge of the group it's placed in. It's either the pixel
squares of the Tangent logo, or a line drawing of a scanned part (a bracket, a wheel rim, a machined plate…).
It's decoration only: it takes no space and screen readers skip it.

## What it looks like

- **Logo**: the small pixel squares of the Tangent logo, fanned out from a corner of the group.
- **Part scans**: a drawing of a part made of small squares, like a 3D scan, in the empty space beside a
  section heading.
- **Handheld scanner**: a drawing of a handheld scanner in the same line style.

Because the mark takes no space, the text doesn't move out of its way. That's why it only goes where the text
leaves the space empty.

## Block settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Drawing** | **Logo** (the pixel squares), one of the part scans (**Part scan: bracket**, **Part scan: CAD part**, **Part scan: cylinder head**, **Part scan: tire**, **Part scan: engine**, **Part scan: machined plate**, **Part scan: wheel rim (wide)**), **Handheld scanner**, or **Auto**. **Auto** draws the part set in the page's **Scan motif** field, and nothing when that field is blank. |
| **Position** | **Top right**, **Middle right (from 990px)**, **Bottom right**, or **In the cell**. **In the cell** isn't a decoration on the edge: the drawing sits in the cell itself, centred, like a picture. |
| **Size** | Only for **Logo**: **Medium** or **Small**. |

### On smaller screens

- The **Logo** is 176 pixels wide (**Medium**) or 128 pixels (**Small**) on large screens, 128 pixels on
  tablets, and hidden on phones (below 750 pixels wide).
- The part scans keep their own size on large screens (from 1200 pixels) and shrink a little on tablets.
  Where there's no room left beside the text, the drawing moves under the text instead of covering it.

## Notes

<Warning>
Use a pixel mark at most once per page, and only where there's empty space. Never place it over text. See
[Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont#layout).
</Warning>

- Place the pixel mark as the **last block** of the group that holds the heading, not straight inside a group
  that draws lines between its blocks (see [Section layout](/blocks/section-layout#group-settings)).
- When you add one, copy it from a page that already has the same kind of section, so the drawing and its
  position match.
