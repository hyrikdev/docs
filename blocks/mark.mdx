## What it does

Draws a faint decoration on the edge of the group it's placed in. It's decoration only: it takes no space and
screen readers skip it.

- **Logo**: the small pixel squares of the Tangent logo, fanned out from a corner of the group.
- **Part scans**: a drawing of a part made of small squares, like a 3D scan (a bracket, a wheel rim, a
  machined plate…), in the empty space beside a section heading.
- **Handheld scanner**: a drawing of a handheld scanner in the same line style.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **location-chicago**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    In the sidebar, open the section that holds it and the groups inside it. On a location page: the
    **Intro and at a glance** section, then the **Intro row** and **Intro heading** groups. Select
    **Pixel mark**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select the group that holds the heading and choose **Add block › Pixel mark**.

## Settings

**Drawing** picks the logo or a part, and **Position** where it sits. **Size** only applies to the logo.

{/* settings:begin source=block:ts-mark level=3 */}

Used 53 times across 53 page layouts and header/footer groups. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Drawing** | 10 options: Logo, Auto, Part scan: bracket, Part scan: CAD part, … | Logo | Logo (46), Part scan: bracket (2), 5 more | Auto draws the part chosen in the page's Scan motif field, or nothing when it's blank. Part scans keep their size on large screens and shrink a little on tablets. |
| **Position** | Top right, Middle right (from 990px), Bottom right, In the cell | Top right | Top right (46), Middle right (from 990px) (5), 2 more | In the cell isn't an edge decoration: the drawing sits centred in its cell, like a picture. |
| **Size** | Medium, Small | Medium | Small | Shown when **Drawing** is Logo. Medium: 176px wide, small: 128px, from 990px; both are 128px between 750 and 989px. Below 750px the drawing is hidden. |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None, except with **Drawing** set to **Auto**: then the drawing comes from the page's **Scan motif** field, and
nothing shows when that field is blank. Only the shared **landing** template uses **Auto**.

## Limitations

<Warning>
Use a pixel mark at most once per page, and only where there's empty space. Never place it over text. See
[Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont#layout).
</Warning>

- The mark takes no space, so the text doesn't move out of its way. That's why it only goes where the text
  leaves the space empty. Where there's no room left beside the text on a smaller screen, a part scan moves
  under the text instead of covering it.
- The logo is hidden on phones (below 750 pixels wide).
- Place the pixel mark as the **last block** of the group that holds the heading, not straight inside a group
  that draws lines between its blocks (see [Section layout](/blocks/section-layout#group-settings)).
- When you add one, copy it from a page that already has the same kind of section, so the drawing and its
  position match.
