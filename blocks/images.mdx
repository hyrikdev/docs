## What it does

The **Image** block shows one picture: a photo beside a block of text, the hero photo of a page, a client logo
or a drawing. Its **Tangent style** decides the look: **Media** for photos, **Logo** for client logos.

The site has 558 Image blocks on 79 page layouts: 448 photos (**Media**), 106 client logos (**Logo**), 3 small
logos in a row and 1 wordmark.

Photos inside cards and galleries aren't Image blocks: they're set on each **Card**. See
[Card list](/blocks/card-list).

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **landing-cnc**). See
    [How the pages are set up](/editing-pages/how-pages-work).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the image">
    Click the picture in the preview, or open its section in the sidebar and select **Image**. Most images sit
    inside a group, next to the text they belong to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it">
    Under **Image**, click **Change** to pick another file or upload one. See
    [Replace an image](/how-to/replace-image).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select a section or group and choose **Add block › Image**.

## Cropping and focus

Most photos are cropped to fill their frame, so every photo in a row has the same height. If the important part
of a photo is cut off:

1. Click the image (or the card).
2. Move **Crop position, vertical**: lower numbers keep more of the top, higher numbers more of the bottom.
3. Check the desktop and the mobile view: the frame's shape changes between them.

If the image is a screenshot, a report or a drawing with text in it, turn on **Show the whole image** instead.
Text in a picture should never be cut off.

## Good images

- **Size:** at least 1600 pixels wide for wide photos, 1000 pixels for cards. Keep files under about 1 MB.
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG or SVG for logos and drawings.
- **One shape per row:** all photos in a row or a grid use the same **Aspect ratio**.
- **Real photos:** use Tangent's own project photos. Don't add stock photos that weren't on the site before.
- **Alt text:** describe each image in a short sentence. The alt text belongs to the file: see
  [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images).
- **Logos:** the alt text is the company name shown on the logo.

## Settings

Pick the picture under **Image** and the look under **Tangent style**: **Media** for photos, **Logo** for client
logos. For photos, **Crop position, vertical**, **Show the whole image** and **Caption** are the ones you may
need. The size, border and padding settings are already set to fit the layout around the image: leave them as
they are.

## Limitations

- **Caption**, **Crop position, vertical**, **Show the whole image** and **Copy first when stacked** only work
  with the **Media** style.
- The frame's shape can change between desktop and phone, so a crop that looks right on one may cut on the
  other. Check both.
- An image connected to a page field shows a placeholder in the theme editor when the field is empty. With
  **Hide when empty** on, visitors see nothing there.
- The block has no alt text setting: the alt text always comes from the file.
- Logos look gray until the visitor points at them; that's the **Logo** style, not a faded file.
