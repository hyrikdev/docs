## What it does

The **Quote form** block is the site's request form. Visitors type their name, email and phone (on most forms
also their company and a description of the project) and click the button. After sending, the form is replaced
by a check mark, a heading ("You're all set.") and a short thank-you text. Fields marked with a star must be
filled in; a short red message under the field says so when one is missing.

The site has 66 quote forms, in four versions:

| Version | Where | Fields | Button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full form | The **Quote** section of the 13 service pages | Name, Phone, Email, Company, Tell us about your project, with gray example text | **Send**, with the note "No obligation. We reply within one business day." |
| Contact form | The contact page, right column, without the gray background | The same five fields | **Send**, with the note "* Required fields" |
| Location form | The **Quote** section of the 46 location pages | Name, Phone, Email, Tell us about your project, no example text | **Send** |
| Short form | The hero of the 5 short landing pages and the shared **landing-short** layout | Full name, Phone, Email | The page's own label, for example "Send now" |

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **service-reverse-engineering**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    On service and location pages, open the **Quote** section at the bottom and select **Quote form** inside its
    groups. On the contact page and the short landing pages, it's inside the **Page** section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select a group and choose **Add block › Quote form**. **Quote form (location)** and
**Quote form (short)** add the location and short versions with their fields already set.

### Jump to the form from a button

Every form has the name `quote`, so a button with the link `#quote` scrolls down to it. The **Get a Quote**
buttons in the heroes of service and location pages work this way: keep their link. The
[mobile call bar](/blocks/sticky-bar), when it's turned on, does the same on pages with a form. See [Buttons](/blocks/buttons).

## Settings

Most changes are words: the field labels, the button label and the messages. Turn fields on or off with
**Company** and **Phone**; empty **Description label** to drop the project description. Leave
**Form name (for tracking)** and **Anchor ID** as they are, and leave **What do you need?** and
**Where should we scan?** off.

Used 66 times across 66 page layouts and header/footer groups. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Form name (for tracking)** | Text | “full” | “location” (46), “service” (13), 2 more | Tells analytics which kind of form a message came from: service, location, short or contact. Keep the value the form already has. |
| **Card style** | On, Off | On | On (65), Off (1) | Puts the form on a light gray background that fills its cell. On for every form except the contact page's. |
| **Anchor ID** | Text | “quote” | “quote” | The name buttons use to jump to the form (#quote). Keep "quote". Use another name only if a page has two forms. |

## Data and metafields

Nothing has to exist in the admin for the form to work. Where its messages go, and the one field it reads:

| Where | What lives there |
|---|---|
| Shopify admin → **Settings → Notifications** | The store's **sender email**. Every message from every quote form is emailed there. Change it there if messages should reach another inbox. |
| The message itself | A hidden **Source page** line with the address of the page it was sent from, so you can see which page brought the request. |
| Short landing pages → the page's **Form button label** field | The button text of the short form. See [Landing pages](/page-recipes/landing-page). |

See [Contact pages](/page-recipes/contact-pages) for the pages built around the form.

<Tip>
After a change to the form or the sender email, send yourself a test message from the live page and check it
arrives.
</Tip>

## Limitations

- The form can't take file uploads. Ask visitors to email photos or drawings, for example in **Photos note**.
- Each block has its own words. To change a label on every location page, change it in every location page's
  form, or ask your developer to do it in one go.
- Keep **Anchor ID** as "quote". Use another name only if a page has two forms; buttons linking to `#quote` then
  reach only the one still called "quote".
- **What do you need?** and **Where should we scan?** have no wording and aren't on the live forms. Leave them
  off.
- The **Add block** list also offers Horizon's **Contact form**. The site doesn't use it: add a **Quote form**
  instead, so the form looks and behaves like the others.
