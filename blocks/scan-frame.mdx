The **Scan frame** block shows one photo as if it were on a 3D scanner's screen: rulers along the edges, a
fine grid, corner marks, and a laser line that sweeps across the picture. Behind the laser, the photo turns into a
point cloud (a picture made of small dots, like raw scan data). A small readout and a strip of file formats
finish the frame.

## What it looks like

- **Rulers** along the top and left edges, a 50-pixel grid over the photo, three small targets and four corner
  marks.
- **The laser line** moves slowly from side to side. On one side of it you see the photo, on the other side
  the same photo as a blue point cloud.
- **The readout**: a small label with a target icon, inside the frame, for example "0.025mm accuracy".
- **The file formats**: a row of small cells under the picture, one per format.

The whole frame is decoration. Screen readers skip it, and the photo has no alt text.

## Block settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Image** | The photo. The same image is used for the photo and for the point cloud. |
| **Readout caption** | The small label in the frame. Screen readers don't read it, so use words that are also elsewhere on the page. |
| **File formats** | The formats in the strip under the picture, separated by commas. Each one gets its own cell. |
| **Laser sweep** | Makes the laser move. When it's off, or for visitors who have asked their device for reduced motion, the laser stays still part of the way across. |
| **Sweep duration** | How long one sweep takes, from 3 to 20 seconds. The homepage uses 9. |
| **Aspect ratio on desktop** | The shape of the frame on large screens: **21:9**, **16:9**, **3:2**, **4:3**, **Square** or **Portrait 4:5**. |
| **Aspect ratio on mobile** | The same, on phones. |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, **Right**) | Space around the frame. |

## Where it's used on the site

Only on the **homepage**, in the **Hero** section, under the title and the intro. It uses:

- a photo of a scanned engine;
- the readout "0.025mm accuracy";
- the formats "STEP, IGES, STL, POINT CLOUD";
- **21:9** on desktop and **4:3** on phones, with a 9-second sweep.

## Notes

- Part of the photo is always shown as dots. Pick a sharp, wide photo with a clear subject.
- To replace the photo, see [Replace an image](/how-to/replace-image).
