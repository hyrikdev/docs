## What it does

Shows one photo as if it were on a 3D scanner's screen, with a laser line that sweeps across it. Behind the
laser, the photo turns into a point cloud (a picture made of small dots, like raw scan data).

- **Rulers** along the top and left edges, a 50-pixel grid over the photo, three small targets and four corner
  marks.
- **The laser line** moves slowly from side to side. On one side of it you see the photo, on the other side
  the same photo as a blue point cloud.
- **The readout**: a small label with a target icon, inside the frame, for example "0.025mm accuracy".
- **The file formats**: a row of small cells under the picture, one per format.

The site has one scan frame, on the **homepage**, in the **Hero** section, under the title and the intro. It
shows a photo of a scanned engine, the readout "0.025mm accuracy" and the formats "STEP, IGES, STL, POINT
CLOUD".

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the **Home page** template (the first one the editor opens).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    In the sidebar, open the **Hero** section and select **Scan frame**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

To replace the photo, pick another **Image** (see [Replace an image](/how-to/replace-image)). **Readout
caption** and **File formats** hold the words in the frame.

Used once across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Image** | Image | — | Set on all | Pick a sharp, wide photo. The same image shows as the photo and, behind the laser, as a blue dot pattern (point cloud). No alt text: it's decoration. |
| **Readout caption** | Text | “0.025mm accuracy” | “0.025mm accuracy” | The frame is decorative and hidden from screen readers, so use text that is also on the page. |
| **File formats** | Text | — | “STEP, IGES, STL, POINT CLOUD” | Comma separated, one cell each. |

## Data and metafields

None. The photo is picked in the block's **Image** setting (see [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images)).

## Limitations

- The whole frame is decoration. Screen readers skip it, and the photo has no alt text. Don't put words in it
  that aren't elsewhere on the page.
- Part of the photo is always shown as dots. Pick a sharp, wide photo with a clear subject.
- When **Laser sweep** is off, or for visitors who have asked their device for reduced motion, the laser stays
  still part of the way across.
