## What it does

A **Card list** shows a set of cards on the site's ruled grid. Each card is a **Card** block inside the list.
The list decides the layout (cards, figures, steps, a photo gallery, quotes…), the columns and the look; each
card holds its own title, text, icon or photo, and link.

<Frame caption="A card list with four cards, each with a bare line icon">
  <img src="/images/card-list-icons.png" alt="Four cards in a row: File creation, Enhancement, Measurement, Compression, each with a small blue line icon" />

It's the most used block on the site: 289 card lists on 74 page layouts, holding about 1,650 cards. Service,
location, landing and company pages all use it.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **service-reverse-engineering**). See
    [How the pages are set up](/editing-pages/how-pages-work).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the list">
    In the sidebar, open the section and select **Card list** inside it. In some sections it sits inside a group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a card">
    Open the list to see its **Card** blocks, or click a card in the preview. The sidebar shows that card's settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select a section or group and choose **Add block › Card list**. The same menu also has ready-made
starts: **Stats strip**, **Numbered steps**, **Image gallery**, **Static cards** and **Static stats strip**.
To add a card, select the list and choose **Add block › Card**, or see
[Add or edit a card in a grid](/how-to/edit-card).

## Layouts

Select the **Card list** and pick a **Layout**. The 11 layouts:

| Layout | What it shows | Use it for | Lists on the site |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cards** | The standard grid: title and text, with an icon or a photo. | Industries, benefits, why-us, options, service cards. | 106 |
| **Stats** | Big figures with a short line under each, for example "0.025mm". | Rows of figures on service pages. | 5 |
| **Numbered steps** | A process, numbered 01, 02, 03… | Process steps on service pages. | 5 |
| **Gallery** | Photos with a short caption under each. | Photo grids on location and service pages. | 83 |
| **Quotes** | Client testimonials: the quote, the logo, the name and the person's line. | Testimonials on location and landing pages. | 41 |
| **Drawing notes** | Short notes under a drawing, in small mono text, in two columns. No icons, photos or links. | Key specs under the part drawing at the top of two service pages, and one list of benefits. | 3 |
| **Entries** | A term and a sentence in each ruled cell, in one column or a grid. | Lists like "What sets us apart", or reasons beside a photo. | 27 |
| **Link cells** | Each card is a link: its title and an arrow. Turn on **Images in link cells** to add the photo. | Rows of services on the short landing pages. | 7 |
| **Check list** | Short lines, each with a blue check mark. | Short lists of features or reasons. | 5 |
| **Tiles** | Short names in large tiles. Turn on **Centred tiles** when the tiles have icons. | Lists of industries, materials or options. | 6 |
| **Note strip** | A label on the left and a title on the right, on a light gray band. | A single highlighted note. | 1 |

In the **Cards** layout, a card with only a title (no text, image, link or label) shows as a small badge with a
check mark.

## Text then its image on phones

When a section has a row of text cards over a row of their photos (for example "What gets delivered"), turn
on **Text cards over their images** and put the cards in reading order: text 1, image 1, text 2, image 2…
Desktop keeps two rows; phones show each text right before its image.

## Settings

On the list, the settings that matter most are **Layout**, **Columns on desktop** and **Columns on mobile**. For
cards with icons, keep **Card icon** on **Bare icon, 24px**. On each card you mostly change **Title**, **Text**,
**Icon** or **Image**, and **Link**. Many of the other switches were set for one particular page: leave them as
they are.

## Data and metafields

None for most lists: the words, icons and links are typed in each **Card** in the theme editor.

| Where | What lives there |
|---|---|
| Theme editor → **Card list** and **Card** blocks | The layout, and each card's title, text, label, icon, photo and link. |
| Shopify admin → **Content → Files** | The photos and their alt text. A card has no alt text setting of its own. See [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images). |
| Shopify admin → **Online Store → Blog posts** | Only for a list with **Blog posts** set and no cards: the latest posts show as cards. See [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts). |

## Limitations

- The grid always ends on a full row: when the cards don't fill the last row, the last cards stretch to fill it.
  Don't add cards just to fill a row.
- A list never shows more columns than it has cards.
- A card with no title, text, image or link shows nothing.
- **Text cards over their images** needs the cards in pairs (a text card, then its photo card), exactly twice as
  many cards as columns, **Show images** on and **Columns on mobile** on 1. Otherwise the list falls back to a
  normal grid.
- **Bento** is designed for 4 columns.
- Leave **Cards** (card entries) empty: no list on the site uses it, and entries chosen there would replace the
  cards typed in the editor.
- A card has an icon or a photo, not both. Photo cards get no icon.
