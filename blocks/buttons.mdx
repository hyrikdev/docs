## What it does

A **Button** block is a link that looks like a button. The site uses square buttons in two looks: black
(**Primary, neutral**) for the main action and white with a line (**Secondary**) for the second one. Call
buttons carry a phone icon; other buttons end in an arrow.

<Frame caption="A call button (black, with the phone icon) and a quote button (white, with an arrow)">
  <img src="/images/breadcrumbs-desktop.png" alt="A location hero with a black 'Call us now' button with a phone icon and a white 'Get a Quote' button with an arrow" />
</Frame>

The site has 290 buttons on 84 page layouts: in heroes, closing call rows, asides and text sections. The most
common are **Call us now** and **Get a Quote** in the heroes of location and service pages.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **location-boston**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    In the sidebar, open the section, then the group that holds the buttons (often **Buttons**), and select the
    **Button**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select a group and choose **Add block › Button**, or **Add block › Call button** for a ready-made
call button.

## Button styles

Every button on the site uses the **Style** under **Tangent style**:

| Style | Looks like | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary, neutral** | Black, white text | The main action, and every call button. |
| **Secondary** | White, with a thin line | The second action, for example **Get a Quote** next to a call button. |
| **Accent** | Blue | Not for ordinary buttons. Blue is kept for selected items and progress. |

**Size** (**Medium**, **Small**, **Large**), **Trailing arrow** and **Full width on mobile** only show once a
Tangent style is picked. The theme-wide **Buttons** settings don't change these buttons: see
[Theme settings › Buttons](/theme-settings/buttons).

### Call buttons and quote buttons

- **Phone first.** When a call button and a contact or quote button sit together, the call button comes first
  and is **Primary, neutral**. The contact or quote button comes second and is **Secondary**.
- **Every call button has the phone icon.** Pick **Tangent phone** as the **Leading icon**. The icon replaces
  the arrow by itself.
- **A button whose label is the phone number** shows only the icon and the number, for example
  "949-345-0611", not "Call 949-345-0611". Labels like "Call Now" or "Call us now" keep their words.
- **Quote buttons** link to `#quote`, which scrolls down to the [quote form](/blocks/contact-and-quote-form) on
  the same page. On a page without a form, link to the contact page instead.
- **Hero buttons on phones** use **Full width on mobile**.

### Add a call button

1. In the sidebar, click **Add block** in the group where the button should go.
2. Choose **Call button**. It comes ready with the label "Call Now", the phone icon, the black style and the
   link `tel:9493450611`.
3. Change the label if needed. Check the number in the link.
4. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
A phone link is written `tel:` followed by the number without spaces, for example `tel:9493450611`. Visitors
on phones can tap it to call.
</Tip>

### Links to other websites

A link to another website (for example a Calendly booking page or a Google Drive folder) opens in a new tab
by itself. You don't need to turn on **Open link in new tab**. Its arrow turns diagonal (↗) so visitors know
they're leaving the site. Links to pages on usetangent.com keep the straight arrow (→) and open in the same tab.

<Frame caption="A button that goes to another website shows the diagonal arrow">
  <img src="/images/buttons-external.png" alt="A black 'Book a call' button with a diagonal arrow" />
</Frame>

This works everywhere on the site: buttons, cards, menus, and links inside text, blog posts and FAQ answers.
Phone and email links are left as they are.

### Link to a page on the site

In **Link**, start typing the page name and pick it from the list. Shopify fills in the right address, for
example `/pages/reverse-engineering`. To jump to the quote form on the same page, type `#quote`.

The settings that matter are **Label**, **Link**, and under **Tangent style** the **Leading icon** and
**Style**. Leave the Horizon **Style** near the top as it is: the Tangent **Style** overrides it.

## Data and metafields

None for most buttons: the label and link are typed in the theme editor. The two hero buttons of the long
landing pages read the page's **Hero button 1 label** / **Hero button 1 link** and **Hero button 2 label** /
**Hero button 2 link** fields: change them on the page in the admin. See [Landing pages](/page-recipes/landing-page).

## Limitations

- A button with an empty **Label** doesn't show at all. That's how a landing page hides a hero button: leave
  its label field empty.
- A button with a **Leading icon** never shows the arrow, even with **Trailing arrow** on.
- Leave the theme-wide **Buttons** settings and the Horizon **Style** alone; they don't restyle the site's buttons.
- Each button is its own block. If the phone number changes, every call button's link needs the new number:
  see [Change the phone number](/how-to/change-phone-number).
