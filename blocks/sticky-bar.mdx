## What it does

On phones and tablets, a bar with two buttons sits at the bottom of the screen: **Call Now** and
**Get a Quote**. When it's on, it shows on every page of the site, and it's the main way phone visitors reach
Tangent.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open any page">
    In the theme editor, open any page, and switch the preview to the mobile view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the bar">
    At the bottom of the sidebar, in the **Footer** group, select **Mobile sticky CTA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on or off">
    Use the eye icon next to **Mobile sticky CTA** to show or hide the bar on the site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it behaves

With **Show the bar** set to **After scrolling**:

- It's hidden at the top of the page, where the hero has its own buttons.
- It slides in once the visitor scrolls past the first screen.
- It slides out while the quote form or the footer is on screen, so it never covers them.

It shows on screens narrower than 750 pixels, and on tablets and touch screens where the header uses the menu
drawer. It doesn't show on desktop screens.

## Data and metafields

None. The store phone in **Settings → Store details** is used only when **Phone number** is empty.

## Limitations

- The phone number is typed here, so it doesn't follow the store phone or the header. Update it with the others:
  see [Change the phone number](/how-to/change-phone-number).
- The bar is the same on every page: you can't turn it off for one page only.
- On phones, the header's **Contact us** button is hidden because this bar is meant to carry the quote button.
  While the bar is off, phones have no quote button at the top or bottom of the screen. See [Header](/blocks/header).
