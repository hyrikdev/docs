On phones and tablets, a bar with two buttons sits at the bottom of the screen: **Call Now** and
**Get a Quote**. It's the main way phone visitors reach Tangent.

  <img src="/images/sticky-bar-phone.png" alt="A bar at the bottom of a phone screen with a black Call Now button and a white Get a Quote button" style={{ maxWidth: "375px" }} />
</Frame>

## How it behaves

- It's hidden at the top of the page, where the hero has its own buttons.
- It slides in once the visitor scrolls past the first screen.
- It slides out while the quote form or the footer is on screen, so it never covers them.
- It doesn't show on desktop screens.

## Where to find it

In the theme editor, it's at the bottom of the sidebar, under **Footer**: **Mobile sticky CTA**. Switch the
preview to the mobile view to see it.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show the bar** | **After scrolling** (the behavior above) or **Always**. Keep **After scrolling**. |
| **Phone number** | The number the **Call Now** button calls. Leave empty to use the store phone from **Settings → Store details**. |
| **Call button label** | "Call Now". |
| **Quote button label** | "Get a Quote". |
| **Quote button link** | Where **Get a Quote** goes when the page has no quote form: `/pages/contact-us`. On pages with a quote form, the button jumps to the form. |
