The announcement bar is the thin blue bar above the header. It currently says "Schedule your free estimate
now" and links to the free estimate booking page.

<Frame caption="The announcement bar. The diagonal arrow shows the link goes to another website.">
  <img src="/images/announcement-bar.png" alt="A blue gradient bar with the mono text 'Schedule your free estimate now' and a diagonal arrow" />
</Frame>

## Where to find it

In the theme editor, it's the first line in the sidebar, under **Header**: **Announcement bar**. Click the
arrow next to it to see the **Announcement** block inside.

## Announcement settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Text** | The message. Keep it short: one line on a phone is about 40 characters. |
| **Link** | Where the bar goes when clicked. A link to another website opens in a new tab and shows the diagonal arrow (↗). |
| **Typography** | Font, size, weight, letter spacing and case. Leave these as they are. |

## Bar settings

Click **Announcement bar** itself to see:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tangent style** | **Brand gradient** gives the blue gradient bar with mono text and the arrow. Keep it. |
| **Time to next announcement** | With more than one announcement, how many seconds each one shows. |

<Tip>
You can add a second **Announcement** block. The bar then rotates between them. One message is clearer, so
keep a second one for short campaigns.
</Tip>

To change the text or link step by step, see [Change the announcement](/how-to/change-announcement).
