## What it does

The announcement bar is the thin blue bar above the header, on every page of the site. It currently says
"Schedule your free estimate now" and links to the free estimate booking page. The site has one announcement.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open any page">
    In the theme editor, open any page. The bar is the same on all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the bar">
    At the top of the sidebar, in the **Header** group, select **Announcement bar** for the bar's own settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the announcement">
    Open **Announcement bar** and select the **Announcement** block inside it to change the text or the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select **Announcement bar** and choose **Add block › Announcement**. With two or more, the bar
rotates between them.

## Settings

You'll only change the **Announcement**'s **Text** and **Link**. On **Announcement bar**, keep **Tangent style**
at **Brand gradient**: it gives the blue gradient, the mono text and the arrow. To change the text or link step
by step, see [Change the announcement](/how-to/change-announcement).

### Announcement

{/* settings:begin source=block:_announcement level=4 */}

Used once across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Text** | Rich text | “Shop our latest arrivals!” | Set on all | The message. Keep it short: about 40 characters fit on one line on a phone. |
| **Link** | Link | — | Set on all | Where the bar goes when clicked. A link to another website opens in a new tab and shows the diagonal arrow (↗). |

## Data and metafields

None. The text and the link are typed in the theme editor.

## Limitations

- The bar shows on every page: you can't show it on some pages only.
- One message is clearer than several. Keep a second **Announcement** for short campaigns, and remove it after.
- A long message wraps onto two lines on phones. Keep it to about 40 characters.
- The **Typography** settings, the text color and the background color have no effect while **Tangent style** is
  **Brand gradient**: the style sets them. Leave them as they are.
