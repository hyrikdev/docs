## What it does

Three small blocks finish the blog pages. None of them holds text of its own to keep up to date: they read the
posts of the blog.

| Block | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Blog tags** | The **Blog** list | A row of filter chips, one per tag. |
| **Load more** | The **Blog** list | A button under the posts that loads the next posts. |
| **Recent posts** | Every article | The latest posts, each with a small picture and its title. |

**Blog tags** is a row of chips above the posts. The first chip shows all posts; each other chip shows only the
posts with that tag. The chip of the list you're on is blue. On the site, the first chip is labelled "Blog" and
the tag "Blog" gets no chip of its own. The case studies list has no tag chips.

  <img src="/images/blog-index.png" alt="The blog page with the title Blog, a row of tag chips and post cards" />
</Frame>

**Load more** is a button under the posts. A click adds the next page of posts under the ones already shown,
without leaving the page. The button disappears after the last page. The case studies list shows up to 50 posts
on one page, with no button.

<Frame caption="Load more at the bottom of the blog list, with the post count">
  <img src="/images/blog-load-more.png" alt="The last row of post cards, then a 'Load more' button with '9 / 85' beside it" />
</Frame>

**Recent posts** is a short ruled list of the latest posts, each with a small picture and its title, linking to
the post. The post you're reading is left out, so the list never links to the page it's on. On the site it's
in the [Sticky column](/blocks/sticky-column) beside every article, under the **Call Now** and **Get a Quote**
buttons, titled "Recent articles".

Each block is used once on the site: **Blog tags** and **Load more** on the **blog** template, **Recent posts**
on the **article** template (so on every post).

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template">
    In the theme editor, open the **blog** template for the blog list, or the **article** template for the
    posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the block">
    On **blog**: open the **Blog posts** section and select **Blog tags** or **Load more**. On **article**:
    open the **Blog post** section, then **Sticky column**, and select **Recent posts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select a section or group and choose **Add block › Blog tags** (or **Load more**, **Recent posts**).

## Settings

Most of what these blocks show comes from the posts themselves. How many posts make one page isn't a setting of
**Load more**: it's **Posts per page** on the **Blog posts** section. The **Blog** list shows 9 posts per page;
at 50 (the most), every post up to 50 is on one page and the button isn't needed.

### Blog tags

{/* settings:begin source=block:ts-blog-tags level=4 */}

Used once across 1 page layout or header/footer group. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Show a chip for all posts first** | On, Off | On | On | Links to the blog without a tag filter. |
| **First chip label** | Text | — | “Blog” | Shown when **Show a chip for all posts first** is on. Leave blank to use the blog title. |
| **Tags to leave out** | Text | — | “Blog” | Tag names separated by commas, for example a tag that repeats the first chip. |

## Data and metafields

| Where | What lives there |
|---|---|
| Shopify admin → **Online Store → Blog posts** | The posts, their tags and their featured images. See [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts). |
| Tags of the posts | The chips of **Blog tags**. A new tag on a post adds a new chip by itself. See [Tags and filter chips](/admin/blog-posts#tags-and-filter-chips). |
| Featured image of each post | The small picture beside each recent post. |

## Limitations

- The chips only list tags that are on posts. To remove a chip, remove the tag from the posts, or add it to
  **Tags to leave out**.
- The blog list and the article are explained page by page in
  [Blog and case studies](/page-recipes/blog-and-case-studies).
