Three small blocks finish the blog pages. None of them holds text of its own to keep up to date: they read the
posts of the blog.

| Block | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Blog tags** | The blog list | A row of filter chips, one per tag. |
| **Load more** | The blog list | A button under the posts that loads the next posts. |
| **Recent posts** | Every article | The latest posts, each with a small picture and its title. |

The posts themselves are written in the Shopify admin. See [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts).

## Blog tags

  <img src="/images/blog-index.png" alt="The blog page with the title Blog, a row of tag chips and post cards" />
</Frame>

A row of chips above the posts. The first chip shows all posts; each other chip shows only the posts with that
tag. The chip of the list you're on is blue.

The chips come from the tags of the posts. A new tag on a post adds a new chip by itself. See
[Tags and filter chips](/admin/blog-posts#tags-and-filter-chips).

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show a chip for all posts first** | Adds a first chip that links to the blog with no tag filter. |
| **First chip label** | The text of that first chip. Leave it blank to use the blog's title. |
| **Tags to leave out** | Tag names, separated by commas, that get no chip. For example a tag that would repeat the first chip. |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**, **Left**, **Right**) | Space around the row. |

On the site: the **Blog** list (template **blog**) uses a first chip labelled "Blog" and leaves out the tag
"Blog". The case studies list has no tag chips.

## Load more

A button under the posts. A click adds the next page of posts under the ones already shown, without leaving the
page. The button disappears after the last page.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Label** | The button text. The site uses "Load more". |
| **Show post count** | Shows how many posts are shown out of the total, for example "9 / 85". |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**) | Space above and below the button. |

How many posts make one page isn't a setting of this block: it's **Posts per page** on the **Blog posts**
section. The **Blog** list shows 9 posts per page. At 50 (the most), every post up to 50 is on one page and
the button isn't needed.

On the site: the **Blog** list only. The case studies list shows up to 50 posts on one page, with no button.

## Recent posts

A short ruled list of the latest posts, each with a small picture and its title, linking to the post. The post
you're reading is left out, so the list never links to the page it's on.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Heading** | The title above the list. |
| **Blog** | Which blog to list. Leave it blank to use the blog of the post being read. |
| **Number of posts** | How many posts to show, from 1 to 10. |
| **Show images** | Shows the small picture of each post. |
| **Padding** (**Top**, **Bottom**) | Space above and below the list. |

On the site: every article (template **article**), in the [Sticky column](/blocks/sticky-column) beside the
post, under the **Call Now** and **Get a Quote** buttons. Its heading is "Recent articles" and it shows 5
posts with their pictures.
