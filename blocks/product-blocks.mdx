## What it does

A product page in Shopify is built from a **Product information** section. Its blocks read the product
you're looking at: its title, description and photos come from the product in the Shopify admin
(**Products**), not from the theme editor. The site has one product, the HandySCAN Black Elite scanner, on the
template **quote**. The site doesn't sell online, so that page has no price and no cart: visitors call or book
a call instead.

<Frame caption="The top of the HandySCAN page: the Product media block on the left, the Details block on the right">
  <img src="/images/product.png" alt="The HandySCAN product page with breadcrumbs, the scanner photo on the left, and on the right the Creaform and Tangent logos, the title, the description and two buttons" />
</Frame>

The **Product information** section is used on two templates: **quote** (the HandySCAN page) and the default
product template, **product**, described [below](#the-default-product-template).

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product template">
    In the theme editor, open the page picker at the top and choose the **quote** product template (the
    HandySCAN page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the section">
    In the sidebar, select **Product information** for the section's own settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a block">
    Open **Product information** to see **Breadcrumbs**, **Product media** and **Details**. Open **Details** to
    see the blocks inside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add a block, select **Details** and choose **Add block**, then the block, for example **Add block › Text**.

## What it looks like

The top of the page is split in two equal halves on the ruled grid, under a breadcrumbs strip:

- on the left, the product's photo (**Product media**);
- on the right (**Details**): the Creaform and Tangent logos, the product title, the description, and the
  **Call Now** and **Schedule a call** buttons. On large screens this half stays in view while the photo
  column scrolls.

Inside **Details**, the HandySCAN page uses:

| Block | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Group** with two **Image** blocks | The Creaform and Tangent logos, with the **Tangent style** **Logo in a row**. |
| **Text** named **Product title** | The product's title from the admin, shown as the page's main heading (**Heading large**). |
| **Text** named **Description** | The product's description from the admin (**Lede, wide**). |
| **Group** with two **Button** blocks | **Call Now** and **Schedule a call**. See [Buttons](/blocks/buttons). |

Below the top come ordinary sections, built from the same blocks as the rest of the site: applications,
photos, benefits, specs, software, portability and a call to action. See
[Product page](/page-recipes/product-page) for each one.

## The default product template

The theme also has a default product template (template **product**), the theme's standard shop layout. A new
product gets it unless you pick another template on the product in the admin. Its blocks are the shop blocks:

| Block | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Price** | The product's price. |
| **Variant picker** | Buttons or drop-downs to choose a size, color or other option. |
| **Buy buttons** | Holds **Quantity**, **Add to cart** and **Accelerated checkout** (express payment buttons). |
| **Disclosures** | Notices set on the product in the admin (Shopify's product disclosures), as fold-out rows. |
| **Recommended products** | A section under the main one, with a **Product card** for each suggested product. |

The site doesn't sell online, so the HandySCAN page uses none of these blocks.

## Settings

On the HandySCAN page, the settings that matter are on **Product information** (**Equal columns** on,
**Sticky add to cart bar** off, **Breadcrumbs strip above the product** on) and **Details** (**Sticky on
desktop** on). **Tangent uses** counts both product templates, so a value with "(1)" is often the HandySCAN
page's and the other the default template's. **Breadcrumbs** has its own page: [Breadcrumbs](/blocks/breadcrumbs).
**Accelerated checkout** has no settings.

## Limitations

<Warning>
Don't add **Price**, **Variant picker** or **Buy buttons** to the HandySCAN page. The page is built to have no
price and no cart.
</Warning>

- To change the title, the description or the photos at the top, edit the product in the admin
  (**Products** → HandySCAN Black Elite). The theme editor shows where they appear but doesn't hold them.
- A new product gets the default template, with a price and a cart button. Don't switch it to **quote** as
  it is: that template holds the HandySCAN page's own text and photos under the top.
- The **Product card**, **Product image** and **Product title** tables count every product card in the theme,
  not only the ones under **Recommended products**.
- To change the text Google shows for the product, see [Pages and SEO](/admin/pages-and-seo#products-and-blog-posts).
