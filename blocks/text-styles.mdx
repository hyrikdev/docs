## What it does

Every heading, label and paragraph on the site is a **Text** block: 1,456 of them, on 91 page layouts and in
the footer. Its look comes from one setting, **Tangent style**: pick a style and the block takes the Tangent
font, size and color for that job. You don't need to touch the font, size or color yourself.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the theme editor, open the page's template (for example **index** for the homepage). See
    [Open a page in the theme editor](/editing-pages/how-pages-work#open-a-page-in-the-theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the text">
    Click the words in the preview, or expand the section and its groups in the sidebar and click the **Text**
    block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the style">
    Under **Typography**, choose a **Tangent style**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add one, select a section or group and choose **Add block › Text**. (**Add block › Heading** adds the same
block with a heading already in it.)

## The Tangent styles

**Tangent style** has 30 choices. The counts are how many Text blocks use each one.

### Headings and labels

| Tangent style | What it looks like | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| **Eyebrow** | Small mono text in gray, with a blue square before and after. | The short label above a centered section heading, such as "What we do" or "Our process" on the homepage. 31, on 10 pages. |
| **Eyebrow, leading mark** | Small mono text with one blue square before it. | Labels above left-aligned headings, and the footer column titles. The most used style: 302, on 58 pages and the footer. |
| **Display (hero title)** | The largest heading, in the blue gradient. | The main title (H1) at the top of a page. Once per page: 65 pages. |
| **Heading large** | A large heading in the blue gradient. | Section headings. 115, on 70 pages. |
| **Heading medium** | A medium heading in the blue gradient. | Section headings in smaller sections. 272, on 69 pages. |
| **Card title** | A black title. | Titles inside cards and cells, such as the homepage service cells. Card titles stay black. 33, on 17 pages. |
| **Row heading** | A large heading in the blue gradient, a step smaller than **Heading large**. | The title of a text-and-photo row, or a title beside its text. 135, on 36 pages. |
| **Statement title** | A big heading in the blue gradient, kept narrow so it breaks over a few lines. | A title beside untitled text, half and half (service pages, short landing pages, our company). 9 pages. |
| **Statement title, long** | The same, a size smaller. | A statement title of more than about 60 characters. 3 short landing pages. |
| **Stat value** | A big figure in the blue gradient, on one line. | The figures of the homepage **Stats** and of **Working to success** on the services page. 8. |
| **Price** | A large figure in black. | A rate under its name, such as "$115 per hour" on the reverse engineering scanner page. 1. |
| **Label** | Small text of medium weight, in black. | Short lines beside icons, such as the facts under the homepage hero or the address and hours on short landing pages. 39, on 31 pages. |
| **Mono** | Small mono text. | Fine print: the copyright line, the author's name on a blog post, the homepage badges. 8, on 3 pages and the footer. |

### None

**None** leaves Horizon's own look, set by **Preset**. Only 8 Text blocks use it, all on Shopify's built-in store
pages (collections, the default product page and the password page). Pick a Tangent style for everything else.

<Tip>
The paired styles (**Row heading** with **Split text**, **Statement title** with **Statement text**) only sit side
by side inside a group whose **Lines** is set to **No lines, stacked on tablet** (see
[Section layout](/blocks/section-layout#lines-between-cells)). To add one, copy the section from a page that
already has it rather than building it from scratch.
</Tip>

## Heading levels

The style controls the look. The heading level (H1, H2, H3) is set inside the text box and tells search engines
how the page is organized.

- Each page has **one H1**: the page title at the top.
- Section headings are **H2**.
- Card titles are **H3**.

## Settings

**Tangent style** is the setting that matters: it sets the font, size and color. **Text** holds the words.
When a Tangent style is set, **Preset** and the font settings under it (**Font**, **Size**, **Case** and the
rest) are ignored, so leave them as they are. **Page title when blank** and **Hide section when blank** matter on
pages whose text comes from a page field.

{/* settings:begin source=block:text level=3 */}

Used 1,456 times across 92 page layouts and header/footer groups. In **Tangent uses**, a number in brackets is how many of those copies use that value.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Text** | Rich text | — | Set on all, 38 from a page field | The words. Set the heading level (H1, H2, H3) inside the text box. Some copies show a page field instead of typed words. |
| **Page title when blank** | On, Off | Off | Off (1,397), On (59) | When this text is empty on a page (for example a page field left blank), the page's title shows here as the main heading (H1). |

## Data and metafields

Most Text blocks hold words typed in the theme editor. A few show a field of the page instead: the hero title,
the intro under it, the eyebrow, the hero points and the form heading on the landing pages, and the title on a
few other pages. Blog post titles come from the post, and the scanner's name and description from the product.
In the editor these blocks show the name of the connected field instead of a text box; edit the words in the
admin. See
[Text that comes from the page's own fields](/editing-pages/how-pages-work#text-that-comes-from-the-pages-own-fields).

## Limitations

- **Preset**, **Font**, **Size** and the other font settings make no difference once a **Tangent style** is set.
- **Text color** doesn't change the blue gradient styles (**Display**, **Heading large**, **Heading medium**,
  **Stat value**, **Statement title** and **Row heading**). Leave it empty everywhere.
- **Hide section when blank** doesn't hide anything in the theme editor, so the section stays there for you to
  fill in. It works on the live site.
- Write in sentence case: the styles don't change capitals. See [Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont#text).
- The short list with blue check marks under some heroes is a separate block: see [Checklist](/blocks/checklist).
