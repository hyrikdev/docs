## What it does

The header is the bar at the top of every page of the site: the Tangent logo, the menu, a black button with the
phone number and a white **Contact us** button. It stays at the top of the screen while visitors scroll. It's
one shared section, so a change here shows on every page at once.

On phones and tablets, the menu folds into a menu button that opens a drawer, and the phone becomes a square
call button.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open any page">
    In the theme editor, open any page. The header is the same on all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the header">
    At the top of the sidebar, in the **Header** group, select **Header** for the bar's own settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a part">
    Open **Header** to see its four blocks: **Logo**, **Menu**, **Phone** and **Quote button**. Select one to
    change it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The four blocks are fixed parts of the header: you can change them, but not add, remove or move them.

## The four parts

- **Logo**: the logo image comes from the theme settings, not from this block. To replace it, open
  **Theme settings** (the gear icon in the editor's left bar) → **Logo and favicon**. The footer logo follows.
  See [Theme settings](/horizon/theme-settings).
- **Menu**: shows the **Main menu** from **Online Store → Navigation**. To change the menu items, edit that
  menu, not the header. See [Menus](/admin/menus).
- **Phone**: the black button with the phone number. See [Change the phone number](/how-to/change-phone-number).
- **Quote button**: the white **Contact us** button. It's hidden on phones, where the
  [mobile call bar](/blocks/sticky-bar) is meant to carry the quote button.

## Settings

The one header setting that matters is **Tangent style** (under **Appearance**): **Technical** gives the thin
bar with the line under it. Keep it, and keep **Show cart icon** off, because the site doesn't sell online. In
the blocks, you'll mostly change the **Phone number** and the **Quote button**'s **Label** and **Link**. Leave
the **Style** settings as they are.

## Data and metafields

| Where | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **Online Store → Navigation** → **Main menu** | The menu links and their submenus. |
| **Theme settings → Logo and favicon** | The logo image. |
| **Settings → Store details** | The store phone, used by the **Phone** block only when its **Phone number** is empty. |

## Limitations

- The header is shared: you can't give one page a different header.
- The phone number is typed in the **Phone** block, so it doesn't follow the store phone. The same number also
  appears in the footer and in the Helpful Links menu. Update them together: see
  [Change the phone number](/how-to/change-phone-number).
- On phones the **Contact us** button is hidden. While the [mobile call bar](/blocks/sticky-bar) is turned off,
  phone visitors see no quote button in the header, only the call button.
- The **Technical** style expects a menu without submenu images. Keep the **Menu** block's **Media type** at
  **None**.
- **Country/Region** and **Language selector** are on, but they only show when the store sells to more than one
  country or region, or has more than one language.
- Leave the settings for search, the cart bubble and the transparent header alone: the site doesn't use them.
