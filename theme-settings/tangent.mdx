## What it does

**Block cart and checkout** is a setting the Tangent layer adds to Horizon, at the top of the **Cart** group. It
turns the theme into a quote-and-call site: nothing can be bought online.

While it's on:

- buy buttons, quick add and the add-to-cart bar of product pages are hidden,
- the cart icon and the cart drawer aren't drawn,
- the cart page sends visitors to the home page and is kept out of search engines,
- cart and checkout forms, links and requests are stopped in the visitor's browser.

It covers every page. The scanner's product page also uses its own **quote** template, which has call and booking
buttons and no buy button. See [Product page](/page-recipes/product-page).

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Cart**. **Block cart and checkout** is the first setting of the group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

This setting is the Tangent design. It switches off Horizon's shop parts whatever the shop groups say: with it on,
the other **Cart** settings, **Product cards** and **Animations › Add to cart** have nothing left to act on. See
[Store features](/theme-settings/store-features).

## Settings

Keep **Block cart and checkout** on. Its row, with the rest of the **Cart** group, is in the table on
[Store features › Cart](/theme-settings/store-features#cart).

<Warning>
Keep **Block cart and checkout** on. The site sells by quote and phone only. If it's turned off, the cart page and
checkout work again, and any product shown with Shopify's default product template gets its buy buttons back.
</Warning>

## Data and metafields

None.

## Limitations

- It's a guard in the visitor's browser. Programs that send orders to Shopify directly never load the page, so they
  aren't stopped. To be safe, the products should also be set up in the admin so they can't be bought online; ask
  your developer.
- The quote forms and the search page aren't affected.
