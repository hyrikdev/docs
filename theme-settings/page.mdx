## What it does

Sets 2 things for every page: the **Background** color behind all sections, and the **Page width**, the widest
the content gets on a large screen.

Both show on every page of the site. Most sections have no background of their own, so the page background
shows through them. The page width is the width of the 2 thin vertical lines (the rails) that frame every
page, from the header down to the footer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Page**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

- **Page width.** The Tangent design redefines **Narrow** as 1400 pixels, so the rails stay in view on a common
  1440-pixel laptop screen. **Normal** and **Wide** keep Horizon's own widths (1920 and 2400 pixels): the rails
  would move off the edges of most screens, and the card pictures, which are sized for 1400 pixels, could look
  soft.
- **Background.** Not overridden: the Tangent blocks sit on this color. But their text, lines and buttons are
  designed for white, so another color makes the pages look wrong.

## Settings

Leave both settings as they are. **Page width** must stay on **Narrow**.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:page level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Color | Palette background | Palette background | Leave as is. The color behind every section of every page. The Tangent blocks are designed on white. |
| **Page width** | Narrow, Normal, Wide | Narrow | Narrow | Keep **Narrow**: the Tangent design sets it to 1400 pixels, the width of the rails. Another value breaks the line-up of rails and columns. |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None.

## Limitations

- **Background** is linked to the **Background** of the [color palette](/theme-settings/color-palette). Changing
  the palette color changes this too.
- The 1400-pixel width belongs to **Narrow** only. There's no setting for the width itself; ask your developer.
