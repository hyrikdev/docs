## What it does

Sets Horizon's default text color, its 4 fonts and its 7 **text presets**: **Paragraph** and **Heading 1** to
**Heading 6**. A preset is a ready-made text size with its line height, letter spacing and case. Horizon's own
blocks pick a preset; the sizes shrink by themselves on smaller screens.

On this site the group has almost no visible effect: every text on the site's pages uses a **Tangent style**,
which brings its own font, size and color.

## What the Tangent design overrides

- **Fonts.** The Tangent styles use Geist for headings, Inter for text and Geist Mono for small labels. These fonts
  come with the theme and aren't listed in the font pickers here. **Body** and **Heading** (both Archivo) are only
  fallbacks, used for a character the Tangent fonts don't have. **Subheading** (Archivo) and **Accent** (IBM Plex
  Mono) aren't used by the Tangent styles at all.
- **Text presets.** A Text block with a **Tangent style** ignores its **Preset**, so it ignores these sizes too.
  The footer menu, the blog post cards and the FAQ questions also have their look set by the Tangent design.
- **Text color.** Every Tangent style sets its own color, so **Text** only reaches text that has no Tangent style.

What the group still reaches: the pages Horizon draws on its own, such as the password page shown while the store
is locked, and Shopify's shop pages, which the site doesn't link to.

## Settings

Leave the group as it is. To make one text bigger or smaller, don't change the presets: pick another
**Tangent style** on that Text block. See [Text styles](/blocks/text-styles).

## Limitations

- The Tangent fonts and sizes can't be changed in the theme editor. Ask your developer.
- A text with its **Tangent style** set to **None** falls back to the presets here and looks out of place next to
  the rest of the page. Keep a Tangent style on every text.
- Changing a preset changes it on every page that uses it, at once.
