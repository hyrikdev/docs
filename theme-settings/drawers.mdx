## What it does

A **drawer** is a panel that slides in from the side of the screen, for example a shop's cart or its filters. This
group sets the background, text and border colors of Horizon's drawers.

On this site the group has no visible effect. The cart drawer isn't drawn at all, because the cart is blocked, and
the shop filters only exist on Shopify's shop pages, which the site doesn't link to.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Drawers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

The menu that opens from the menu button on phones and tablets is a Horizon drawer, but the Tangent header redraws
it: it opens under the header bar instead of sliding in from the side, with the Tangent white background, no edge
line and numbered rows. These settings don't reach it. See [Header](/blocks/header).

## Settings

Leave the group as it is.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:drawers level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background color** | Color | Palette background | Palette background | Horizon's slide-out panels: the cart and the shop filters, both unused. The phone menu uses the Tangent colors instead. |
| **Text color** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette foreground | — |
| **Border color** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette Color 2 | The line along the panel's edge. The phone menu has no edge line. |

{/* settings:end */}
