## What it does

Sets the line weight (**Stroke**) of Horizon's small interface icons. On this site those are:

- the phone icon of the call button in the header,
- the menu button and its close cross on phones and tablets,
- the plus and caret signs of the [FAQ](/blocks/faq) lists and of the phone menu's sub-menus.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Icons**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

The Tangent line icons keep their own fixed weight and ignore this setting: the icons on cards, the phone icon and
arrow inside Button blocks, and the **Icon** blocks. Changing **Stroke** makes the Horizon icons above thinner or
heavier than the Tangent icons next to them, so the two kinds no longer match. See
[Icons](/design-rules/icons).

## Settings

Leave **Stroke** on **Default**.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:icons level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stroke** | Thin, Default, Heavy | Default | Default | Line weight of Horizon's small icons: the header phone icon, the menu button, the FAQ plus signs. Tangent card and button icons keep their own. |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None.

## Limitations

- The weight of the Tangent icons can't be changed in the theme editor.
