## What it does

Sets the look of the small **Sale** and **Sold out** labels that Horizon puts on product cards in shop lists:
where they sit on the card, their corners, colors and font.

The site shows no product cards and sells nothing online, so no badge appears anywhere on it. The group has no
visible effect.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Badges**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

Nothing: the Tangent blocks have no badges of their own that read these settings. The blue check squares on some
cards are Tangent **Icon** blocks (style **Badge**), not shop badges. See [Icon](/blocks/icon).

## Settings

Leave the group as it is.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:badges level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Position on cards** | Bottom left, Top left, Top right | Top left | Top right | Sale and sold-out badges show on product cards in shop lists. The site shows none, so this group has no visible effect. |
| **Corner radius** | 0–100px | 40px | 100px | — |

### Colors

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sale badge background** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette background | — |
| **Sale badge text** | Color | — | Palette foreground | — |
| **Sold out badge background** | Color | Palette background | #EEF1EA | — |
| **Sold out badge text** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette foreground | — |

### Typography

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Font** | Body, Subheading, Heading, Accent | Body | Body | — |
| **Case** | Default, Uppercase | Default | Default | — |

## Data and metafields

None.

## Limitations

- The badges would only show on Shopify's default shop pages (collections, search results), which the site
  doesn't link to.
