## What it does

Sets the look of the form fields Horizon draws: background, text and border colors, border thickness, corners and
the text preset inside the field.

On this site the group has no visible effect on the pages people visit. The site's forms are the Tangent quote
forms, which ignore it. Horizon's own fields only appear on pages the site doesn't link to, such as the search
page.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Input fields**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

The quote form (on the contact pages and in the closing quote sections) draws its fields in the Tangent design:
white fields with a grey line, 2-pixel corners, small mono labels, a blue line when a field is in use and a red
line when it's filled in wrong. None of it comes from this group. See
[Contact and quote form](/blocks/contact-and-quote-form).

## Settings

Leave the group as it is.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:input-fields level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Color | Palette background | #FFFFFF | Form fields Horizon draws itself, such as the search page field. The quote form uses the Tangent design and ignores this group. |
| **Text** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette foreground | — |
| **Borders** | Color | Palette foreground | #7F8BA3 | — |
| **Border thickness** | 0–4px | 1px | 1px | — |
| **Corner radius** | 0–32px | 8px | 8px | — |
| **Text preset** | Default, Paragraph, Heading 1, Heading 2, Heading 3, Heading 4, Heading 5, Heading 6 | Paragraph | Paragraph | — |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None.

## Limitations

- The email field of the password page (shown only while the store is locked with a password) sets its own colors
  on its block, so this group barely reaches it either.
- Checkout and customer account pages are drawn by Shopify, not by the theme. These settings don't reach them.
