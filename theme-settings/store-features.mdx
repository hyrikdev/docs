## What it does

Six theme settings groups are for selling online: **Cart**, **Prices**, **Product cards**, **Search**,
**Swatches** and **Variant pickers**. Tangent sells by quote and phone, so none of them has a visible effect on the
site. This page lists them in one place so you know what they are and why to leave them alone.

The **Cart** group also holds **Block cart and checkout**, the Tangent setting that keeps the site quote-only.
It has its own page: [Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent).

Where each group would apply, and why it doesn't:

| Group | Where Horizon would use it | Why it doesn't show on the site |
|---|---|---|
| **Cart** | The cart page and the cart drawer. | The cart is blocked by [Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent), and the header has no cart icon. |
| **Prices** | Prices on product pages, product cards and in the cart. | The scanner's product page shows no price. |
| **Product cards** | Product tiles in collection lists, search results and product recommendation rows. | The site has no shop lists. Quick add is also hidden by **Block cart and checkout**. |
| **Search** | The search panel that opens from the header's search icon. | The header has no search icon, so the panel never opens. |
| **Swatches** | Color or picture swatches for product options. | The scanner's product page has no option picker. |
| **Variant pickers** | The option buttons (size, color …) of a product page. | The scanner's product page uses the **quote** template, which has none. |

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a group">
    Click **Cart**, **Prices**, **Product cards**, **Search**, **Swatches** or **Variant pickers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

- [Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent) switches off the cart, quick add and the add-to-cart bar,
  whatever the other **Cart** settings and **Product cards** say.
- The site's own lists (blog posts, case studies, service and location cards) are Tangent blocks. They don't read
  **Product cards** or **Search**.
- The scanner's product page is built from Tangent blocks on the **quote** template, without price, option picker
  or buy button. See [Product page](/page-recipes/product-page).

## Settings

Leave all six groups as they are, and keep **Cart › Block cart and checkout** on: see
[Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent).

### Cart

{/* settings:begin source=theme:cart level=4 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Block cart and checkout** | On, Off | On | On | Keep on. Hides buy buttons, quick add, the cart icon and the cart, sends the cart page home, and stops cart and checkout requests in the browser. |
| **Type** | Page, Drawer | Page | Drawer | No effect while Block cart and checkout is on: the cart drawer isn't drawn and the cart page goes to the home page. |
| **Product title case** | Default, Uppercase | Default | Default | — |
| **Price font** | Body, Subheading, Heading, Accent | Subheading | Subheading | — |
| **"Add to cart" auto-opens drawer** | On, Off | Off | Off | Shown when **Type** is Drawer. |

#### Cart features

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Allow note to seller** | On, Off | Off | Off | — |
| **Open note to seller by default** | On, Off | Off | Off | Shown when **Allow note to seller** is on. |
| **Allow discounts in cart** | On, Off | On | On | — |
| **Installments** | On, Off | On | On | — |
| **Accelerated checkout buttons** | On, Off | On | On | Express payment buttons, such as Shop Pay, in the cart. Not shown while Block cart and checkout is on. |
| **Empty cart button link** | Link | /collections/all | /collections/all | Where the button of an empty cart goes. Not shown while Block cart and checkout is on. |

#### Product media

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Border style** | None, Solid | None | None | — |
| **Border thickness** | 0–10px | 1px | 1px | Shown when **Border style** is not None. |
| **Border opacity** | 0–100% | 50% | 50% | Shown when **Border style** is not None. |

## Data and metafields

None. Products, prices and collections live in the Shopify admin, but the site only uses the scanner's product
record: see [Product page](/page-recipes/product-page).

## Limitations

- Shopify still serves its standard shop pages: a search page, collection pages and the default product template.
  The site doesn't link to them, and they keep Horizon's look rather than the Tangent design. Leave them as they are.
