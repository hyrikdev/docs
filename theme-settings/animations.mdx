## What it does

Turns 4 of Horizon's movements on or off: a fade between pages, a move from a product card to its product page,
a small animation when something is added to the cart, and an effect when the pointer is over a product or
collection card.

On this site **Page transition** is off and **Card hover effect** is **None**. A page transition was tried during
the build and turned off again. The other 2 settings are for shop features the site doesn't use.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Animations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

The small movements of the Tangent blocks, such as the arrow that slides right when the pointer is over a button,
belong to the Tangent design and have no setting here. **Card hover effect** doesn't reach the Tangent cards: it
only applies to Horizon's product and collection cards.

## Settings

Leave the group as it is.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:animations level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Page transition** | On, Off | On | Off | Leave off. A fade between pages; it was tried during the build and turned off again. |
| **Product card to product page transition** | On, Off | On | Off | Shop feature: from a product card to its product page. The site shows no product cards. |
| **Add to cart** | On, Off | On | On | Shop feature. No effect while Cart › Block cart and checkout is on. |
| **Card hover effect** | None, Lift, Scale, Zoom | Lift | None | Product and collection cards only, which the site doesn't show. The Tangent cards ignore it. |

{/* settings:end */}

## Data and metafields

None.

## Limitations

- **Add to cart** shows as on, but has no effect: the cart is blocked by
  [Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent).
- Turning **Page transition** on changes how every page opens. It was turned off on purpose; ask before changing it.
