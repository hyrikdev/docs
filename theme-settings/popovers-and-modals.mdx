## What it does

A **popover** is a small panel that opens over the page next to what you clicked; a **modal** is a window that opens
in the middle of the screen. This group sets their background, text color, corners, border and shadow.

Horizon uses them for search suggestions, the country and language picker, the list of store policies, quick add on
product cards and the add-to-cart bar of product pages. None of these show on the site today, so the group has no
visible effect.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Popovers and modals**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

- The header's drop-down menus on large screens are drawn by the Tangent header: square panels with their own
  shadow. These settings don't reach them.
- The full-screen photo viewer of the project galleries is a Tangent part. See [Gallery and lightbox](/blocks/gallery).

Why the Horizon pop-ups don't show:

| Pop-up | Why it doesn't show |
|---|---|
| Search suggestions | The header has no search. |
| Quick add, add-to-cart bar | Hidden by [Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent). |
| Store policy list | The footer's **Utilities** section that holds it is hidden. |
| Country and language picker | The header shows it only when the store is set up for more than one country or language. Today it isn't. |

## Settings

Leave the group as it is.

{/* settings:begin source=theme:popovers-and-modals level=3 */}

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background color** | Color | Palette background | Palette background | Horizon's small pop-up panels: search suggestions, country picker, policy list, quick add. None show on the site today. |
| **Text color** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette foreground | — |
| **Corner radius** | 0–16px | 8px | 14px | — |
| **Border color** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette Color 2 | — |
| **Border thickness** | 0–10px | 0px | 1px | — |
| **Drop shadow** | On, Off | On | On | — |
| **Shadow** | Color | Palette foreground | Palette foreground | Shown when **Drop shadow** is on. |

{/* settings:end */}
