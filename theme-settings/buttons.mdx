## What it does

Sets the look of Horizon's own buttons: colors, border, corners, font and case for the **Primary button** and the
**Secondary button**, and the corners of **Pills**, the rounded tags of shop filters and search suggestions.

The values here (yellow primary buttons with rounded corners, among others) come from an earlier version of the
design and don't show on the site. All 290 Button blocks on the site use a Tangent **Style**, which ignores this
group: square black **Primary, neutral** buttons and white **Secondary** buttons with a thin line.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Theme settings">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    Click **Buttons**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Tangent design overrides

When a Button block's Tangent **Style** is set, the button drops Horizon's button look completely and takes the
Tangent one: colors, 2-pixel corners, Inter font, height and the trailing arrow. Nothing in this group reaches it.
The same goes for the quote form's send button and the blog's **Load more** button, which are Tangent buttons, and
for the header's phone and **Contact us** buttons, whose colors, corners and font the Tangent header sets over
Horizon's.

So this group only reaches buttons that Horizon draws on its own: on Shopify's shop pages and on the password page,
none of which the site links to.

## Settings

Leave the group as it is. To change how one button looks, change its Tangent **Style** on the Button block. See
[Buttons](/blocks/buttons).

**Tangent uses** is the value saved in the live theme's settings.

### Primary button

| Setting | Values | Default | Tangent uses | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Color | Palette foreground | #E8C204 | Horizon's own primary buttons only. Buttons with a Tangent **Style** ignore every setting in this group. |
| **Text** | Color | Palette background | #13213F | — |
| **Borders** | Color | Palette background | #E8C204 | — |
| **Border thickness** | 0–4px | 0px | 0px | — |
| **Corner radius** | 0–100px | 100px | 8px | — |
| **Font** | Body, Accent | Body | Body | — |
| **Text case** | Default, Uppercase | Default | Default | — |

### Secondary button

## Limitations

- A Button block with its Tangent **Style** set to **None** falls back to the Horizon button set here (yellow and
  rounded for a primary) and looks out of place. Keep a Tangent **Style** on every button.
- The Tangent button colors can't be changed in the theme editor. Ask your developer.
