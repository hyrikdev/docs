---
title: "Change the announcement"
description: "Change the text or link of the blue bar at the top."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. Any page will do: the bar is the same on every page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the announcement">
    In the sidebar, under **Header**, click the arrow next to **Announcement bar**, then click
    **Announcement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the text">
    Edit **Text**. Keep it short and in sentence case, for example "Schedule your free estimate now".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the link">
    Edit **Link**. Paste a full address for another website (it opens in a new tab), or pick a page of the site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and save">
    Look at the bar on desktop and in the mobile view: the text should fit on one line on a phone. Click
    **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
To remove the bar for a while, hide the **Announcement bar** section with the eye icon instead of deleting
it.
</Tip>

See [Announcement bar](/blocks/announcement-bar) for all the settings.
