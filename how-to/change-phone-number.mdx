---
title: "Change the phone number"
description: "Every place the phone number appears, and how to change it."
---

The phone number appears in many places. Some are shared by every page; others are on each page.

## Shared places (change once)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Store details">
    In the Shopify admin, go to **Settings → Store details** and update the phone number. Blocks whose phone
    field is empty use this number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Header phone button">
    In the theme editor, open **Header** → **Phone** and change **Phone number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mobile call bar">
    Open **Footer** → **Mobile sticky CTA** and change **Phone number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Footer contact text">
    Open **Footer**, find the "Contact us" **Text** block and change the number. Click the number, use the link
    button, and update the link too: `tel:` followed by the new number, for example `tel:9495550123`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Footer menu">
    In the admin, go to **Online Store → Navigation → Helpful Links**. Click **Call Now** and change its link to
    `tel:` plus the new number. Click **Save menu**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check">
    Click **Save** in the theme editor. Check the header on desktop and the call bar on a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Places on each page

Most service, location and landing pages also have the number in:

- **Call buttons**, such as "Call us now" or a button showing the number. The number is in the button's **Link**
  (`tel:…`) and sometimes in its **Label**.
- **Contact details** blocks, in the closing section of the page, in the **Phone** field.
- The page's own body text in the admin, on a few plain pages and blog posts.

There are over 80 pages with their own call buttons and contact details. Changing them one by one in the
theme editor takes a long time.

<Warning>
**For a full change of the number, ask your developer.** They can update all page layouts in one go and check
that nothing was missed. Change the shared places above yourself in the meantime, so the header, the call bar
and the footer are right straight away.
</Warning>

## Change one page by hand

1. Open the page in the theme editor (see [How the pages are set up](/editing-pages/how-pages-work)).
2. Click each call button and update **Link** (and **Label** if it shows the number).
3. Click the **Contact details** block and update **Phone**. If you leave it empty, it uses the store phone.
4. Click **Save**.
