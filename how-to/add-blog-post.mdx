---
title: "Add a blog post"
description: "The short version. See Blog posts for the details."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="New post">
    In the Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Blog posts** → **Add blog post**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Type the **Title** (sentence case) and the **Content**. Use **Heading 2** for subheadings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to Tangent pages correctly">
    Link to service pages with their short address, for example `/pages/reverse-engineering`. Links to other
    websites open in a new tab by themselves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Excerpt and image">
    Write a short **Excerpt** and add the featured image with alt text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Blog and tags">
    Pick the **Blog** (News or Case studies) and add one or two existing **Tags**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search engine listing">
    Set the page title and meta description under **Search engine listing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Set **Visibility** to **Visible** and click **Save**. Open the post on the site to check it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Full details: [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts).
