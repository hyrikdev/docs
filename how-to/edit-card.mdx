---
title: "Add or edit a card in a grid"
description: "Change a card's text, icon or photo, or add a new card."
---

## Edit a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Open the page in the theme editor. See [How the pages are set up](/editing-pages/how-pages-work).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the card">
    Click the card in the preview. The sidebar opens its **Card** settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change what you need">
    Edit **Title**, **Text** or **Label**. Change **Icon** or **Image** if needed (a card has an icon or a
    photo, not both).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Check the desktop and the mobile view, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate a card next to it">
    In the sidebar, find a card in the same **Card list**. Open its menu (the **…** button, or right-click it)
    and choose **Duplicate**. The copy has the same settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the copy">
    Change its **Title**, **Text**, **Label** and **Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the icon">
    If the cards have icons, pick the icon for the new card's idea from the [icon table](/design-rules/icons).
    Don't reuse an icon that already means something else on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it in place">
    Drag the card up or down in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Check the grid on desktop and on a phone, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The grid always ends on a full row: when the cards don't fill the last row, the last cards stretch to fill it.
That's expected. Don't add cards just to fill a row.
</Note>

<Warning>
For a grid of texts over matching photos (**Text cards over their images** turned on), cards come in pairs:
a text card, then its photo card. Add both, in that order, or the layout falls back to a normal grid.
</Warning>

## Remove a card

Click the card, then **Remove block** at the bottom of its settings. Or hide it with the eye icon to keep it
for later.
