---
title: "Add a FAQ question"
description: "Add a question and answer to a page's FAQ list."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the page's category">
    In the theme editor, open the page and click its **FAQ category** block. The **Category** setting shows
    the category name. Note it down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the FAQ entries">
    In the Shopify admin, go to **Content → Metaobjects** and click **FAQ**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an entry">
    Click **Add entry**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    - **Question**: in sentence case, ending with a question mark.
    - **Answer**: a short, clear answer. You can add links and lists.
    - **Category**: pick the category you noted in step 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The question appears at the end of the page's FAQ list straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the page">
    Open the page on the site and click the new question to see the answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A page without a FAQ yet

1. In **Content → Metaobjects → FAQ category**, click **Add entry**, type a **Name** (for example the page's
   name) and save.
2. Add the questions as above, with this new category.
3. In the theme editor, open the page. Add a section with an **FAQ category** block, or copy the FAQ section
   from another page, and pick the new category in **Category**.
4. Click **Save**.

See [FAQ](/blocks/faq) and [FAQ entries](/admin/faq-entries) for more.
