---
title: "Replace an image"
description: "Swap a photo on a page and keep it looking right."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the photo">
    Use a JPG at least 1600 pixels wide (1000 for a card), under about 1 MB, with a clear file name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Open the page in the theme editor and click the photo in the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new image">
    Under **Image**, click **Change**, then **Upload** to add the new photo, or pick one already uploaded.
    Click **Select**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the crop">
    If the important part is cut off, move **Crop position, vertical**. If it's a screenshot or a drawing with
    text, turn on **Show the whole image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check both views">
    Look at the desktop and the mobile view. The frame shape can differ between them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add alt text">
    In the admin, go to **Content → Files**, click the new image and fill in **Alt text**. See
    [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Replacing a photo in one place doesn't change it elsewhere. If the same photo is used on several pages, change
it on each page.
</Tip>
