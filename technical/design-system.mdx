The rules below were set by the site owner during the build and are checked in every QA pass. The source is
`docs/agents/BOARD.md` (rules and decisions), `docs/ux/SPACING.md` (spacing) and `assets/tangent-tokens.css`
(tokens). The staff version is [Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont); keep the two consistent.

## Rules

### Build

1. **Blocks first.** Every part a merchant could move is a theme block or a setting on one.
2. **Extend before creating.** Add a setting to an existing block before writing a new one. New settings
   default to the current look. Never rename or remove a setting id.
3. **Horizon conventions** in new code: `content_for 'blocks'`, private `_` blocks, `{{ block.shopify_attributes }}`,
   `"tag": null`, `{% doc %}`, presets, `t:` keys, `spacing-style`. Don't name a snippet parameter `size`.
4. **CSS** in `{% stylesheet %}`, tokens only (no raw colors), Horizon breakpoints, `prefers-reduced-motion`
   respected.
5. **JS** as ES modules with an `@theme/<name>` import-map alias, custom elements extending `Component`.
6. **Content is usetangent.com's.** No invented text, alt, label, caption or heading, no shortened titles. Images
   go through `image_picker` and Shopify Files. Consistency changes may restructure a section but never add
   items to fill a grid or drop content; an empty cell is fine.

### Look

7. **Ruled grid with rails.** 1px lines, rails at the page width, mono labels with accent squares. Lines stay
   continuous: no panel sits a few pixels inside a line, and backgrounds fill whole cells.
8. **Accent `#0562ef` for selection and progress**, headings (through the gradient) and icons. Primary buttons
   are neutral, not blue.
9. **Square shapes.** Radius 2px for controls and panels, 4px for surfaces (`--radius-*`); no pill buttons, no
   boxed or shadowed content.
10. **Gradient titles, black card titles.** Display, large and medium headings use `--ts-ramp-ink`; card titles
    (`heading_sm`) stay solid.
11. **Sentence case everywhere**, words unchanged. Menu titles and the "Call Now" / "Get a Quote" labels keep the
    old site's case. Every casing or typo fix is logged in `migration/content/CHANGES-text.md`.
12. **Fonts:** Geist (display), Inter (body), Geist Mono (labels).

### Columns and dividers

13. **Two-column rows split 50 / 50.** A head | content row splits in thirds (the head a third), a call-to-action
    row is three equal columns (a two-cell CTA gives the copy two thirds).
14. **Column dividers line up** from section to section on shared lines of the frame (k/n of the frame width).
    `migration/scripts/check_dividers.py` checks this from the template JSON.
15. **Don't stack two blocks of the same shape** back to back (for example two identical card grids). Vary the
    rhythm.

### Icons

16. **Card grids carry bare icons.** Industry, application, benefit, why, option and compare grids show an icon
    on every card in the bare form: 24px, accent, no border, no tile (**Card icon** = **Bare icon, 24px**,
    `ts_icon_style: bare`). Never a boxed icon in these grids.
17. **Same concept, same icon.** The homepage icons are the source of truth; a concept that has an icon there
    uses it everywhere. Industry icons come from the `INDUSTRY_ICONS` map in
    `migration/scripts/service_layouts.py` (the generators share it). A title with no mapped icon needs a design
    decision; never reuse an icon that already means another concept.
18. **Cards with a photo take no icon.** Deliverables, capabilities, photo grids, quotes and logos have no icons.

### Phones

19. **One text edge.** Below 750px every heading, eyebrow, card title and caption starts on the page text inset
    `--ts-text-inset` (20px inside the frame, so text starts at x = 37 on a 375px screen). A leading marker
    (square, tick, icon) sits on the edge with the text inline after it.
20. **Text, then its image.** A row of N text cards over a row of N matching images reads text 1, image 1,
    text 2, image 2 below 750px. Put them in reading order in the source (card list **Text cards over their
    images**, `ts_pairs`); desktop keeps the rows by grid placement.
21. **Full-width hero buttons**, 50px tall below 750px (button **Full width on mobile**, `ts_full_mobile`).
22. **Touch targets** are 44px (`--minimum-touch-target` in `snippets/theme-styles-variables.liquid`), except the
    footer link rows, 36px below 750px (`--ts-footer-link-row` in `sections/footer.liquid`).
23. Card text is 15px below 750px; FAQ answers and the homepage hero lede stop at 65ch.

### Links and buttons

24. **External links open in a new tab** with `rel="noopener"`, and their arrow turns 45° (↗, `.ts-arrow-ne`).
    External means an http(s) URL whose host isn't `usetangent.com`, `www.usetangent.com` or the myshopify
    domain. Internal links keep →; `mailto:` and `tel:` are untouched. It's done in theme code, never by
    rewriting store content. See [Architecture](/technical/architecture#external-links).
25. **Phone first.** Where a call and a contact action sit together (hero, header, sticky bar, CTA), the call
    comes first and is primary; contact is second and secondary.
26. **Every call button shows the phone icon.** A button whose label is the phone number shows the icon and the
    number only. A button with a leading icon has no trailing arrow.
27. **Grid foot.** When a grid ends with one onward link, use a secondary button centred 32px below the grid,
    full width below 750px.

### Section heads and decoration

28. **Centred section heads from 750px.** A standalone head (eyebrow + heading + lede alone in its row) is
    centred, with the eyebrow's two squares. Heads beside content, heads with art beside them, heroes and the
    closing form row stay on the start edge. Below 750px all heads sit on the text edge (rule 19). The
    generators centre heads by setting the head group's alignment; there's no extra CSS or setting.
29. **Decoration is rare.** The dot lattice behind a head appears only on the "Why" head. Logo-derived and
    scan-part drawings (**Pixel mark**) appear at most once per page, faint, in empty space, never over text;
    the scan-part drawings only on the 3D scanning pages.

### Images

30. Photos in one row share one shape. Screenshots, reports and drawings with text are shown whole
    (`ts_contain` / `image_contain`), never cropped. Alt text is the old site's alt; one alt per file.

## Tokens

Defined in `assets/tangent-tokens.css` on `:root` (light) with a `[data-theme='dark']` variant. Components read
the semantic roles, never raw values.

| Group | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Neutral roles | `--background`, `--surface`, `--surface-raised`, `--surface-muted`, `--foreground`, `--text-secondary`, `--text-muted`, `--border`, `--border-subtle`, `--border-strong` |
| Accent | `--accent` (`#0562ef`), `--accent-strong` (`#074db7`), `--accent-subtle`, `--accent-foreground` |
| Brand ramps | `--ts-ramp-1/2/3` (accent, logo cyan `#3cb7dd`, lavender), `--ts-ramp`, `--ts-ramp-line`, `--ts-ramp-ink` (title gradient), `--ts-wash` (hover wash on linked cells) |
| Status | `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger` |
| Shape | `--radius-control: 2px`, `--radius-panel: 2px`, `--radius-surface: 4px`, `--radius-pill: 2px` |
| Type | `--font-display` (Geist), `--font-body` (Inter), `--font-mono` (Geist Mono); `--text-xs` 0.75rem … `--text-5xl` 4.5rem; `--tracking-display: -0.03em`; `--leading-display: 1.1`, `--leading-body: 1.4` |
| Space | `--space-1` 0.25rem, `--space-2` 0.5rem, `--space-3` 0.75rem, `--space-4` 1rem, `--space-6` 1.5rem, `--space-8` 2rem, `--space-12` 3rem, `--space-16` 4rem, `--space-24` 6rem |
| Controls | `--control-height-sm` 2.25rem (36px), `--control-height-md` 2.75rem (44px), `--control-height-lg` 3.125rem (50px) |
| Motion | `--ease-enter`, `--ease-standard`, `--ease-exit`; `--duration-instant` 0.12s … `--duration-considered` 0.48s |
| Layout | `--ts-max: 1400px`, `--ts-gutter: clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px)`; `--narrow-page-width: 87.5rem` (in `snippets/ts-tokens.liquid`) |

The older `--ts-*` names (`--ts-ink`, `--ts-text`, `--ts-line`, `--ts-accent`, `--ts-mono`…) are aliases of these
roles in `snippets/ts-tokens.liquid`. Use the Arc roles in new code. Shadows (`--shadow-*`) exist in the token set
but the design doesn't use shadowed content.

## Spacing

Spacing comes from Horizon's own padding and gap settings. Inside a Tangent frame (`ts_frame`), Horizon's
scale variables are retuned (`snippets/section.liquid`, same in `sections/footer.liquid`):

- a padding of 20 or less is used as is; a larger value becomes `max(20px, scale × value)`;
- a gap of 24 or less is used as is; a larger value becomes `max(24px, scale × value)`.

| Width | below 750 | 750–989 | 990–1199 | 1200 and up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scale | 0.42 | 0.65 | 0.85 | 1 |

Every spacing setting on a Tangent page uses one of seven values:

| Name | Setting | 375 | 768 | 1024 | 1440 | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gap-sm` | gap 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | Title → body in a card, buttons in a row |
| `gap-md` | gap 16 | 16 | 16 | 16 | 16 | Eyebrow → heading → lede; gaps between separate cards |
| `gap-lg` | gap 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 | Icon or image → copy in a card |
| `pad-sm` | padding 24 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 24 | Compact cells (hero facts, badges, copyright) |
| `pad-cell` | padding 48 | 20 | 31 | 41 | 48 | Inline padding of every row and cell inside the rails; block padding of content cells |
| `pad-head` | padding 64 | 27 | 42 | 54 | 64 | Heading cluster → grid |
| `pad-section` | padding 96 | 40 | 62 | 82 | 96 | Top of a section that opens with a heading cluster |

- **Left edges line up.** With `pad-cell` inline padding everywhere, text starts at x = 37 / 72 / 82 / 89 at
  375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440.
- **Vertical rhythm.** `pad-section` above a head, `gap-md` inside it, `pad-head` from the head to the grid. A
  ruled grid ends on its own bottom line, with no section padding below it.
- **Balanced cells.** Content cells use the same padding on all four sides.
- **Continuation sections** (no `ts_rule`) that continue the lines above start with top padding 0.
- Keep gaps at 24 or less unless they should scale between widths.

Fixed exceptions: gallery captions 12 block / 16 inline; stat cells 16 inline; the announcement bar 6 / 6.
The full spec, with measured values, is `docs/ux/SPACING.md`.

## Breakpoints and widths

| Breakpoint | Meaning |
|---|---|
| below 750px | Phones. Text inset rule, stacked rows, full-width buttons. |
| 750px | Tablet layout starts (the most used breakpoint in the Tangent CSS). |
| 990px | Desktop layout starts. From here Horizon scrolls the page inside `.page-wrapper`. |
| 1200px | Wide desktop: full spacing scale, some grids switch to their desktop columns (card list **Desktop columns from** `1200`). |

The page is 1400px wide at most (`--ts-max`, `--narrow-page-width`), which leaves the rails visible on a 1440px
screen (`docs/ux/SPACING.md` measures them at x = 40 and 1400). A few decorative drawings use their own widths (1280, 1300, 1420) to decide when they stack
under the text instead of sitting beside it. Use the Horizon breakpoints (750 / 990 / 1200) for anything else.

## Type styles

The **Text** block's **Tangent style** (`ts_style`) adds a `ts-text--<value>` class; the styles are in the
`{% stylesheet %}` of `snippets/text.liquid`:

| Value | Label | Look |
|---|---|---|
| `eyebrow`, `eyebrow_start` | **Eyebrow**, **Eyebrow, leading mark** | Geist Mono 0.8125rem, secondary color, 5px accent squares on both sides (or only before) |
| `display` | **Display (hero title)** | Geist 500, `clamp(2.375rem, 4.6vw, 3.75rem)`, at most 31ch, gradient. One per page. |
| `heading_lg` | **Heading large** | `clamp(2rem, 4.4vw, 3.25rem)`, at most 44rem, gradient |
| `heading_md` | **Heading medium** | `clamp(1.5rem, 2.6vw, 2.25rem)`, gradient |
| `heading_sm` | **Card title** | 1.375rem, solid |
| `lede` | **Lede** | `clamp(1rem, 1.4vw, 1.125rem)`, secondary color, at most 38rem |
| `lede_wide` | **Lede, wide** | 1rem, at most 50rem (65ch in the homepage hero) |
| `body` | **Body small** | 0.875rem, secondary color |
| `prose` | **Long-form text** | Reading style for long bodies kept from the old site |

`assets/tangent-tokens.css` has matching shared classes (`.ts-eyebrow`, `.ts-heading--xl/lg/md/sm`, `.ts-lede`,
`.ts-body`) for blocks that print their own markup.

The list also has `label`, `stat`, `mono`, `chips`, `chips_large`, `chips_check`, `statement`,
`statement_long`, `statement_text`, `heading_row`, `prose_columns`, `split_text`, `closing_line`,
`step_names` and more; see the schema in `blocks/text.liquid`. The staff guide is
[Text styles](/blocks/text-styles).

## Icons

- The Tangent icons are values with a `ts_` prefix in Horizon's icon lists (56 today: `ts_scan`, `ts_target`,
  `ts_truck`, `ts_plane`, `ts_car`…), drawn as 24px stroked SVG by `snippets/icon-or-image.liquid` and
  `snippets/icon.liquid`, with the newer ones in `snippets/ts-icon-more.liquid`. The prefix avoids clashes with
  Horizon's own icon names.
- They appear in the **Icon** block, the button's **Leading icon** and the card's **Icon**.
- The paths are `when 'ts_…'` branches in `snippets/icon.liquid` (first set) and `snippets/ts-icon-more.liquid`
  (newer ones). The value lists are in three schemas: `blocks/icon.liquid`, `blocks/button.liquid` and
  `blocks/_card-list-item.liquid`.
- To add one: draw it on the 24px grid as a stroke-only path, add its `when` branch, add the value with a
  **Tangent …** label to all three schemas (keep the `ts_` prefix), and add its row to the staff
  [icon table](/design-rules/icons).

## Motion

- Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Scroll reveal (`.ts-reveal`) is CSS only; browsers without scroll-driven animations show the content as is.
- No count-up numbers. Horizon's own animation settings are used; page transitions are off.
