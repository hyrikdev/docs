title: "Sections and blocks"
description: "The two kinds of building parts every page is made of."
---

Every page on the site is a stack of parts. Shopify uses two words for them:

- **Section**: a horizontal band of the page, from one edge to the other. For example the homepage has a
  "Hero" section, a "Services" section, a "Process" section and so on.
- **Block**: a piece inside a section, such as a heading, a paragraph, an image, a button or a card.
  Blocks can sit inside other blocks. A **Group** block holds other blocks together, for example one card
  with its icon, title and text.

## How they appear in the theme editor

In the left sidebar of the theme editor:

- Each section is a line with its name, for example **Services** or **Industries**.
- Click the small arrow next to a section to see the blocks inside it.
- Blocks inside blocks are indented one step further.
- A block shows its type or its text, for example **Text**, **Button**, **Card list** or the first words of a
  heading.

Click any line to open its settings. You can also click the part directly in the preview.

## Why this matters

Because every part is a block, you can:

- change the words and images of one block without touching the rest;
- drag blocks up or down to change their order;
- hide a block without deleting it;
- add a new block from the same list of blocks the designer used.

See [Editing content](/editing-pages/editing-content) for how to do each of these.

<Tip>
Before you add something new, look at a page that already has what you need and copy its setup. The site
looks consistent because the same blocks and settings are used everywhere.
</Tip>
