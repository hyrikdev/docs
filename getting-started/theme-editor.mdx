The theme editor is where you change what visitors see on the site.

## Open it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the Shopify admin">
    Use your own staff account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the themes">
    In the menu on the left, click **Online Store**, then **Themes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the theme">
    The theme at the top, marked as the current theme, is the one visitors see.
    Other themes in the **Theme library** are drafts that visitors don't see.
    Click **Customize** next to the theme you want to edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Changes you save on the current theme go live at once. Read
[Preview and publish](/getting-started/preview-and-publish) before you make big changes.
</Warning>

## The editor screen

The editor has three areas:

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Top bar** | Shows which page you're editing. Click the page name in the middle to switch to another page. It also has the desktop / mobile view switch, undo and redo, and the **Save** button. |
| **Left sidebar** | Lists the parts of the page from top to bottom: the header, the page sections and the footer. Click a part to open its settings. |
| **Preview** | Shows the page as visitors will see it. Click anything in the preview to open its settings. |

When you click a part, its settings open in a panel. Changes show in the preview straight away, but they're
not saved until you click **Save**.

## Check the phone view

Many visitors use phones. After each change, switch the preview to the mobile view with the phone icon in the
top bar and check the page there too.
