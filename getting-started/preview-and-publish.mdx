title: "Preview and publish"
description: "Always look at a change before visitors do."
---

A Shopify store can hold several themes, but only one is live at a time. Shopify calls it the
**current theme**. The others sit in the **Theme library**, where only staff can see them.

## Two ways to work

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Small fix on the live site">
    For a typo, a new phone number or a swapped photo:

    1. Open the current theme with **Customize**.
    2. Make the change.
    3. Check it in the desktop and the mobile view.
    4. Click **Save**. The change is live right away.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bigger change">
    For a new section, a reordered page or several pages at once, work on a copy first:

    1. Go to **Online Store → Themes**.
    2. Next to the current theme, click the **…** button, then **Duplicate**.
       The copy appears in the **Theme library**.
    3. Click **Customize** on the copy and make your changes. Save as often as you like:
       visitors don't see the copy.
    4. To see the copy as a visitor would, click the **…** button next to it, then **Preview**.
    5. When you're happy, click **Publish** on the copy. It becomes the current theme.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
A copy is a snapshot. If someone changes the live theme while you work on a copy, publishing your copy
replaces their change. Agree with your team who edits what, and keep copies short-lived.
</Warning>

## Always preview first

- Look at every page you changed, in the desktop and the mobile view.
- Click the buttons and links you added. Links to other websites should open in a new tab.
- Read the text once more for typos and casing. The site uses sentence case: see the
  [design rules](/design-rules/do-and-dont).

## The new design and the old one

The new Tangent design was built on a theme of its own. Until that theme is published, the live site
still shows the old design. See [The page shows the old design](/help/troubleshooting#the-page-shows-the-old-design).
