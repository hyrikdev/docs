The site has two blogs, **News** (shown as "Blog", at /blogs/news) and **Case studies** (/blogs/case-studies).
Three templates lay them out: **blog** for the blog list, **case-studies** for the case studies list, and
**article** for every single post in both blogs. The posts themselves (title, text, picture, excerpt, tags,
date) are written in the Shopify admin, not in the theme editor. See [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts).

<Note>
In Shopify a blog is a list of posts, and each post is called an **article** or a **blog post**. The theme
editor only sets how the list and the posts look, plus the blocks around them.
</Note>

## Where each part comes from

| What you see | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Post title | The post's **Title** in **Online Store → Blog posts**. |
| Post text | The post's **Content**. |
| Picture on the card and at the top of the post | The post's featured **Image**. |
| Short text on the card | The post's **Excerpt**. If it's empty, the start of the post text is used. |
| Date on the card and the post | The post's publish date. |
| Tag on the card and the post, and the filter chips | The post's **Tags**. The first tag shows on the card and the post. |
| Breadcrumbs, the "Blog" title, buttons, recent articles, author box, closing call | The theme editor (templates **blog**, **case-studies** and **article**). |

## The blog list (blog)

<Frame caption="The blog list with its tag chips">
  <img src="/images/blog-index.png" alt="The blog page with the title Blog, a row of tag chips and post cards" />
</Frame>

The blog list is one section, **Blog posts**. From top to bottom:

| Block | What you see | Settings to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Breadcrumbs** | Home / Blog. | **Title override** is "Blog", because the blog's own title in the admin is "News". |
| **Group** with a **Text** | The title "Blog" (**Display (hero title)**). | Typed. |
| **Blog tags** | A row of filter chips, one per tag, with a first "Blog" chip for all posts. | **First chip label** and **Tags to leave out**. See [Blog blocks](/blocks/blog-blocks). |
| The post cards | One ruled cell per post: picture, date and first tag, title, and the excerpt cut to three lines. The whole card is a link. | The card's **Tangent style** is **Ruled cell**. |
| **Load more** | A **Load more** button with the count of posts shown, for example "9 / 85". | **Label** and **Show post count**. |

The section shows 9 posts at a time (**Posts per page** on the **Blog posts** section). **Load more** adds the
next 9 below them without leaving the page.

<Frame caption="The bottom of the blog list: Load more and the count of posts shown">
  <img src="/images/blog-load-more.png" alt="The last row of three post cards, then a 'Load more' button with the count '9 / 85' beside it" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
A new tag on a post adds a new chip. Reuse the existing tags so the row of chips stays short. See
[Tags and filter chips](/admin/blog-posts#tags-and-filter-chips).
</Tip>

## The case studies list (case-studies)

The case studies list uses the same **Blog posts** section with fewer blocks, and a closing call:

| Section | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Blog posts** | Breadcrumbs (Home / Case studies), the title "Case studies" and the post cards. There are no tag chips and no **Load more** button: the section shows up to 50 posts on one page. |
| **Call to action** | "Tell us about your project", a short text, the phone button and **Send a message**. |

## The article page (article)

<Frame caption="The top of an article: breadcrumbs, date, title and picture">
  <img src="/images/article.png" alt="An article page with the breadcrumbs Home / Blog, the date, a large blue title and the featured photo" />
</Frame>

Every post in both blogs uses the **article** template. It has two sections.

### Blog post section

| Block | What you see | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Breadcrumbs** | Home / Blog / post title. | **Parent label** "Blog" renames the News blog's crumb. A case study shows its own blog's title instead. |
| **Details** | The date and the first tag, in small mono text. | The post's date and tags. The author is turned off. |
| **Group** with the title | The post title, large and blue. | The post's **Title**. |
| **Featured image** | The post picture in a wide 21:9 frame. | The post's **Image**. |
| **Content** | The post text, followed by the author box: a photo, a name and a short bio. | The text is the post's **Content**. The author box is a group of blocks inside **Content**, typed in the theme editor. |
| **Sticky column** | **Call Now**, **Get a Quote** and "Recent articles", which stay in view beside the text while visitors scroll. On phones they come after the text. | Typed buttons and a **Recent posts** block. See [Sticky column](/blocks/sticky-column). |

**Recent posts** lists the five latest posts of the same blog, each with a small picture, and leaves out the post
being read. See [Blog blocks](/blocks/blog-blocks).

### Call to action section

The same closing call as the case studies list: "Tell us about your project", the phone button and
**Send a message**.

<Warning>
The author box and the buttons are part of the **article** template, so they show on every post in both blogs.
A change there changes all posts at once.
</Warning>

## What's safe to change

- **Posts**: write, edit, tag and hide them in the admin at any time. See [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts).
- **Chips**: leave a tag out of the chips with **Tags to leave out** on the **Blog tags** block.
- **Buttons and the author box** on the article template: change their words in the theme editor, knowing that
  every post follows.
- **Don't** change the card's **Tangent style** or the number of **Posts per page** without checking the
  **Load more** button still works on the live blog.
