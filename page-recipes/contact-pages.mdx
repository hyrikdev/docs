Two pages exist to start a conversation: the contact page (/pages/contact-us, template **contact**) with the
contact details and the quote form, and the free consultation page (/pages/free-consultation, template
**free-consultation**) with a booking calendar and a map. Both are edited in the theme editor; only the free
consultation hero reads the page record.

## Where form messages go

Every form on the site, on the contact page, the service and location pages and the short landing pages, is a
**Quote form** block. It's a standard Shopify contact form:

- Each message is emailed to the store's **sender email**. You set it in the Shopify admin under
  **Settings → Notifications**. Change it there if messages should reach another inbox.
- Each message carries a hidden **Source page** line with the address of the page it was sent from, so you can
  see which page brought the request.
- The form can't take file uploads. Visitors who want to send photos or drawings should email them.

See [Contact details and quote form](/blocks/contact-and-quote-form#quote-form) for every setting of the form.

<Tip>
After a change to the form or the sender email, send yourself a test message from the live page and check it
arrives.
</Tip>

The whole page is one section, **Page**. There's no closing call below it: the form is the call.

| Part | What you see | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| **Breadcrumbs** | Home / page, in its own ruled row. | The **Breadcrumbs** block. |
| **Get in touch** group (left) | The label "Get in touch", the title "Let's discuss your project", an intro, the phone button and **Schedule a meeting**, then the rows Phone, Email, Address and Office hours. | **Text** blocks (**Eyebrow, leading mark**, **Display (hero title)**, **Lede**), a **Buttons** group with two **Button** blocks, and a [Contact details](/blocks/contact-details) block. |
| **Send us a message** group (right) | "Send us a message", a short text, and the form. | **Text** blocks (**Card title**, **Body small**) and the **Quote form**. |

Everything is typed in the theme editor, including the phone, email, address and hours in **Contact details**.
The address row links to Google Maps through **Address link**.

### The contact form

The form on this page is the full quote form:

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes |
| **Phone** | No |
| **Email** | Yes |
| **Company** | No |
| **Tell us about your project** | Yes |

Under the button it shows "* Required fields". After sending, the visitor sees "You're all set!" and a thank-you
text: change both under **Messages** in the form's settings.

**Contact details** block on the contact page, and the other pages that list them. See the warning in
[Contact details and quote form](/blocks/contact-and-quote-form#contact-details).

## What's safe to change

- **Texts, buttons and contact details**: click the block, change it, save.
- **Form labels and messages**: in the **Quote form** settings. Keep **Anchor ID** as "quote": buttons (and the
  mobile call bar, when it's turned on) use it to jump to the form.
- **Logos** in **Clients**: replace, add or remove **Image** blocks, keeping the **Logo** style.
- **Don't** turn on **What do you need?** or **Where should we scan?**: those questions aren't part of the
  site's forms.
