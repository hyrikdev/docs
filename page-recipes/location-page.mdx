Each location page, such as 3D scanning Chicago, has its own template named **location-** plus the page's
address. There are 46 of them. A location page presents Tangent's services for one city or area and ends on
the quote form. All of its words and photos are typed in the theme editor, in that page's template. The
locations overview (/pages/locations) uses one more template, **locations-hub**, described at the end of this
page.

## Three layouts

The 46 templates come in three layouts. All of them start with **Location hero** and end with **Quote**.

| Layout | Templates | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **City page** | 33 | Chicago, Atlanta, Boston, Phoenix, Seattle, Hawaii |
| **Service in a city**, with content rows | 12 | 3D scanning services California, 3D scanning services Irvine, 3D mobile laser scanning Las Vegas, Las Vegas |
| **3D laser scanning services**, a layout of its own | 1 | /pages/3d-laser-scanning-services |

The city page layout has two versions: 23 pages have an **Industries** section, 9 don't. Hawaii adds content
rows and a **FAQ** section. Look at the sidebar of the template you open to see which version it is.

## City page, top to bottom

This is the layout of **location-chicago** and most city pages.

| # | Section in the sidebar | What visitors see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Location hero** | Breadcrumbs, the title, the intro, **Call us now** and **Get a Quote**, a photo |
| 2 | **Intro and at a glance** | An eyebrow, a heading, an intro, and four cards with icons |
| 3 | **Gallery** | A heading and a grid of project photos |
| 4 | **CAD row** | A photo beside a heading, text, a checked list (on most pages) and a button to file examples |
| 5 | **Services** | A heading and cards with photos, each linking to a service page |
| 6 | **Industries** | A heading and industry photos with captions (on 23 of the 33 city pages) |
| 7 | **Client quotes** | A heading and three client testimonials |
| 8 | **Projects** | A heading and rendering photos with captions |
| 9 | **Quote** | The heading, the contact details and the quote form |

### 1. Location hero

**Built from:**

- **Breadcrumbs**, Tangent style **Strip: own ruled row**, with **Parent label** "Locations" and **Parent
  link** `/pages/locations`. The last step is the page's **Title** in the admin.
  See [Breadcrumbs](/blocks/breadcrumbs).
- A **Hero row** group set to **Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**: the **Hero text** group (the
  title in **Display (hero title)**, the intro in **Lede**, and a **Buttons** group) and an **Image**
  (Tangent style **Media**).

**Safe to change:** the title, the intro and the photo. **Call us now** links to `tel:` plus the number;
**Get a Quote** links to `#quote`, the form at the bottom: keep that link.

### 2. Intro and at a glance

**Built from:** an **Intro row** group (**Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**) with the **Intro
heading** group (**Eyebrow, leading mark**, **Heading medium** and a **Pixel mark**, the faint logo squares)
and the **Intro text** group (**Lede**). Under it, a **Card list**: layout **Cards**, 4 columns, **Card
style** **Plain**, **Card icon** **Bare icon, 24px**. Each **Card** has a title, a short text and an icon.

**Safe to change:** the words and icons. Use the icon that matches the idea (see [Icons](/design-rules/icons)).
Keep four cards so the row stays full. The pixel mark appears only here: don't add another on the page.

### 3. Gallery

**Built from:** a **Heading medium**, then a **Card list**: layout **Gallery**, 4 columns (2 on phones),
**Bento** on, photos at 4:3. Each photo is a **Card** with only an **Image**. Bento makes the first photo
large and the last ones wide so the grid ends on a full row. See [Card list](/blocks/card-list).

<Frame caption="The Chicago gallery: Bento makes the first photo large and fills every row">
  <img src="/images/location-gallery.png" alt="The heading 'Chicago 3D scanning services' above a four-column photo grid: one large photo top left, then rows of scanning photos with no gaps" />
</Frame>

**Safe to change:** the photos, and how many there are. Bento fills the rows by itself. Give every photo alt
text in **Content → Files**.

### 4. CAD row

**Built from:** a **CAD row** group (**Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**) with an **Image** on the
left and the **CAD copy** group on the right: **Heading medium**, a **Lede, wide** paragraph, a **Label**
("Benefits of…"), a **Checklist** block, another **Lede, wide** paragraph and a **Button**. On 11 city
pages the label and the checklist aren't there: the row has two paragraphs and the button.

- The **Checklist** holds its lines in **Items**, one per line. See [Checklist](/blocks/checklist).
- The **Click for file examples** button links to a shared folder on another website, so it opens in a new
  tab with the diagonal arrow.

<Frame caption="The CAD row on the Chicago page, with its checklist">
  <img src="/images/location-cad-row.png" alt="A rendering of a suspension arm on the left; on the right the heading 'Chicago 3D scanning for CAD', a paragraph, the label 'Benefits of Chicago 3D scanning service', six checked items, a line of text and the 'Click for file examples' button with a diagonal arrow" />
</Frame>

**Safe to change:** the words, the list lines and the photo.

### 5. Services

**Built from:** a **Heading** group with a **Heading medium**, then a **Card list**: layout **Cards**,
4 columns (2 on phones), photos at 4:3, **Default link label** "See details". Each **Card** has a title, an
image and a **Link** to a service page, for example `/pages/cad-design-services`.

**Safe to change:** the cards' titles, photos and order. Link each card to the right service page with its
short address.

### 6. Industries

**Built from:** a **Heading** group with a **Heading medium**, then a **Card list**: layout **Gallery**,
4 columns (2 on phones), photos at 4:3. Each **Card** has an industry name as its title and a photo.

**Safe to change:** the names and photos. Keep the photos the same shape.

### 7. Client quotes

**Built from:** a **Heading** group with a **Heading medium**, then a **Card list**: layout **Quotes**,
3 columns. In each **Card**, **Text** is the quote, **Title** the person's name, **Label** the company and
**Image** the client's logo.

**Safe to change:** use real client quotes only, word for word. Don't invent or shorten a quote.

### 8. Projects

**Built from:** a **Heading** group with a **Heading medium**, then a **Group** set to **Grid with lines**
(4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet and phones). The first block is an **Image** with **Grid span** set to
**Lead, 2 columns, fills the row**. Each other project is a **Group** (**Lines between blocks**) with an
**Image** and a caption in **Eyebrow, leading mark**.

<Frame caption="The Chicago projects grid: a lead photo over two columns, then photos with captions">
  <img src="/images/location-projects.png" alt="The heading 'Take a look at recent 3D laser scanning projects' above a grid: a wide photo of a scanner on the left, then scan renderings captioned 'Tire 3D scan 37 inch tire rendering', 'Dirt bike rendering', 'Motor rendering', 'Bod Pod rendering', 'UTV rendering' and 'Jeep rendering'" />
</Frame>

**Safe to change:** the photos and captions. Keep one shape (4:3) for every photo so the rows line up.

### 9. Quote

**Built from:** a **Quote row** group (**Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**): the **Quote text**
group with a **Heading large** and a **Contact details** block, and the **Form** group with the **Quote
form** block (its **Form name (for tracking)** is "location"). See
[Contact details and quote form](/blocks/contact-and-quote-form).

**Safe to change:** the heading. The phone number is typed in the **Contact details** block's **Phone**
field. Leave the form's **Anchor ID** as `quote`.

## Service in a city pages

These pages (for example 3D scanning services California or 3D mobile laser scanning Phoenix) keep the
first three sections of a city page (**Location hero**, **Intro and at a glance**, **Gallery**) and the
**Quote** at the end. In between, they use these sections, in an order that follows each page's text:

| Section in the sidebar | What it's built from |
|---|---|
| **Band** | A short **Card title** on the left; a **Row text** and a **Button** on the right |
| **Content row** | A photo with a caption on one side; on the other an **Eyebrow, leading mark**, a **Row heading**, sometimes a **Closing line**, a **Row text** and a **Button**. The photo side alternates from row to row |
| **Content rows** | Several text and photo rows in one section, built with a **Card list** |
| **Industries**, **Services**, **Client quotes** | The same as on city pages, on some of these pages |
| **Closing** | A **Heading medium**, a **Lede** and a **Button**, with the **Dot lattice** pattern |

Each content row is its own section, so you can reorder rows by dragging the sections in the sidebar.

## 3D laser scanning services

**location-3d-laser-scanning-services** has a layout of its own: **Location hero**, **Industries**, **Our 3D
scanning process**, **Technology**, **Benefits** (a **Card list**, layout **Entries**, with bare icons),

## Where the words come from

| What | Where to change it |
|---|---|
| Headings, texts, cards, photos, buttons | Theme editor, in the page's own **location-** template |
| The last breadcrumb | The page's **Title** in **Online Store → Pages** |
| FAQ questions (Hawaii only) | **FAQ** entries in the admin. See [FAQ](/blocks/faq) |
| Search title and description | The page's **Search engine listing**. See [Pages and SEO](/admin/pages-and-seo) |
| The link on the locations overview | The **Locations** menu. See [Menus](/admin/menus#locations-menu) |

<Note>
Location pages are separate: a change in **location-chicago** changes only Chicago. To change the same text
on many location pages, ask your developer, who can update all the templates at once.
</Note>

## The locations overview

The locations overview (/pages/locations) uses the **locations-hub** template. It shows the title, Tangent's
address, phone, email and office hours, and a list of every location page in ruled columns.

<Frame caption="The locations overview: the contact details come from the page's Content, the list from the Locations menu">
  <img src="/images/locations-hub.png" alt="The locations overview: breadcrumbs, the title Locations, the address, phone, email and office hours, then city names in three ruled columns" />
</Frame>

It has one section, **Page**, with one **Locations hub** group (**Lines between blocks**) inside:

| Block | What it shows | Where the words come from |
|---|---|---|
| **Breadcrumbs** | Home / Locations | The page title |
| **Hero** group with a **Title** text block (**Display (hero title)**) | The title "Locations" | Connected to the page's **Title** in the admin |
| **Page text** group with a **Content** block | Address, phone, email and office hours | The page's **Content** box in **Online Store → Pages**. **Short h3 headings as labels** turns its small headings into the mono labels |
| **Locations** (**Menu** block) | Every location in three ruled columns, with a caption and the nested pages under each city | The **Locations** menu in **Online Store → Navigation**. **Tangent style** is **Index: ruled columns**; **Caption from the page title** adds the small line under a city |

The section has the **Dot lattice at the end** pattern behind the title.

**Safe to change:** the contact text (in the page's **Content**) and the list (in the **Locations** menu).
Nothing on this page needs the theme editor for everyday changes.
