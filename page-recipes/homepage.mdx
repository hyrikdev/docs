The homepage uses the **Home page** template, the first template the theme editor opens. It introduces
Tangent Solutions and sends visitors to a call or a message. All of its words, icons and photos are typed in
the theme editor: nothing on it comes from the page record, a blog or a metaobject.

## The sections, top to bottom

| # | Section in the sidebar | What visitors see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | The page title, the intro, the call and message buttons, the scan picture and four short facts |
| 2 | **Logo grid** | "Trusted by industry leaders" and twelve client logos |
| 3 | **Services** | "What we do" and six service cells, each with an icon |
| 4 | **Process** | The five steps from part to CAD file, playing one after the other |
| 5 | **Stats** | Four big figures, such as 0.025mm |
| 6 | **Why Tangent** | Three reasons with icons, then five short checked lines |
| 7 | **Capabilities** | A heading, an intro and six project photos with captions |
| 8 | **Scanning options** | In-house, shop and mobile scanning, each with check chips |
| 9 | **Call to action** | "Tell us about your project" with the two buttons again |

Every section has the Tangent frame: **Rails, no inner padding**, and a **Top border across the page**
except the hero. See [Section layout](/blocks/section-layout).

<Note>
Unlike service and location pages, the homepage doesn't use **Card list** blocks. Each card is a **Group**
holding an **Icon** and **Text** blocks. To change a card, open its group and click the block inside.
</Note>

## 1. Hero

**What you see:** the main heading "Precision 3D scanning services and reverse engineering", an intro, the
phone button and **Send a message**, a wide scan picture with a moving laser line, and a row of four facts
with icons (0.025mm accuracy, In-house + mobile scanning, Same-day service available, Serving aerospace,
auto, medical and more).

**Built from:**

- A **Group** with the title: a **Text** block, Tangent style **Display (hero title)**.
- A **Group** with **Lines** set to **Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**: the intro (**Text**,
  **Lede, wide**) on the left and a group with two **Button** blocks on the right.
- A **Scan frame** block: the photo, the readout caption and the strip of file formats under the picture.
  See [Scan frame](/blocks/scan-frame).
- A **Group** with **Lines** set to **Ruled grid, compact cells on phones** (4 columns on desktop, 2 on
  tablet and phone). Each of its four groups holds an **Icon** (Tangent style **Accent**) and a **Text**
  (**Label**).

**Safe to change:** the words, the buttons' labels and links, the scan frame's photo, caption and formats,
and the facts. Keep the title the only **Display (hero title)** text on the page.

<Tip>
The scan frame is decorative and hidden from screen readers. Its caption should repeat something already on
the page, such as "0.025mm accuracy".
</Tip>

## 2. Logo grid

**What you see:** the eyebrow "Trusted by industry leaders", the heading "Serving major industries" and a
grid of client logos.

**Built from:** a **Group** with an **Eyebrow** and a **Heading medium** text, then a **Group** set to
**Grid with lines** (6 columns on desktop, 4 on tablet, 3 on phones) holding twelve **Image** blocks with
Tangent style **Logo**.

**Safe to change:** swap a logo by clicking its **Image** block and choosing another image. Keep twelve logos
so the grid fills two full rows on desktop. Add alt text to each logo (the client's name). See
[Images](/blocks/images).

## 3. Services

**What you see:** "What we do", the heading "3D scanning and engineering services", an intro and six cells:
3D laser scanning, Reverse engineering, CAD design services, Dimensional inspection, Legacy part
documentation and On-site mobile scanning.

**Built from:**

- A **Group** for the section head: **Eyebrow**, **Heading large**, **Lede**, and a **Pixel mark** (a faint
  part drawing on the right, from 990 pixels wide).
- A **Group** set to **Grid with lines** (3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on phones). Each cell is a
  **Group** with an **Icon** (**Accent**) and a group with a **Card title** and a **Body small** text.

**Safe to change:** the words of each cell, and its icon from the **Tangent** icons. The homepage icons are
the reference for the whole site, so change one only on purpose. See [Icons](/design-rules/icons).

## 4. Process

**What you see:** "Our process", "From physical part to CAD file in 5 steps", and five steps (Scope, Scan,
Process, Rebuild, Deliver). The active step shows its photo; the steps advance by themselves.

  <img src="/images/process.png" alt="The heading 'From physical part to CAD file in 5 steps' above a photo with the counter '01 / 05' and the list of five steps, with Scope open and its text showing" />
</Frame>

**Built from:** a **Group** for the head (**Eyebrow** and **Heading large**), then a **Group** with
**Lines between blocks** holding a **Process** block. The **Process** block holds five **Process step**
blocks, each with a **Number** (01. to 05.), **Title**, **Text** and **Image**. On the **Process** block,
**Autoplay** is on and **Time per step** is 6 seconds. See [Process](/blocks/process).

**Safe to change:** each step's title, text and photo. To add or remove a step, change the heading too
("in 5 steps"). Keep **Accessible name** on the **Process** block the same as the heading.

## 5. Stats

**What you see:** four figures: 0.025mm accuracy, 24–48 hours processed and delivered, 2–5 business days,
800 sq ft dedicated warehouse.

**Built from:** a **Group** set to **Grid with lines** (4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on phones).
Each cell is a **Group** with a **Text** in **Stat value** and a **Text** in **Eyebrow, leading mark**.

**Safe to change:** the figures and the lines under them. Use only figures Tangent can stand behind.

## 6. Why Tangent

**What you see:** a centred head "Why engineers choose Tangent Solutions for 3D scanning" with a faint dot
pattern behind it, three reasons (Experience, Precision, Responsiveness) and a row of five checked lines
(Fast turn-around, Same day service, Expert technicians, Universal file delivery, In-house teams).

<Frame caption="Why Tangent: three reasons with line icons, then the five checked lines">
  <img src="/images/why-badges.png" alt="The centred head 'Why engineers choose Tangent Solutions for 3D scanning' over a faint dot pattern, three reasons (Experience, Precision, Responsiveness) with blue line icons, and a row of five blue check badges" />
</Frame>

**Built from:**

- The section's **Background pattern** is **Dot lattice**, and its content is centred.
- A **Group** for the head: **Eyebrow**, **Heading large**, **Lede**.
- A **Group** set to **Grid with lines** (3 columns): each reason is a **Group** with an **Icon** (**Accent**)
  and a group with a **Card title** and a **Body small** text.
- A **Group** set to **Grid with lines** (5 columns on desktop, 1 on tablet and phones): each line is a
  **Group** with an **Icon** (**Tangent check**, Tangent style **Badge**) and a **Text** in **Mono**.

**Safe to change:** the words of the reasons and the checked lines. The dot pattern is the only decoration of
its kind on the page; don't add another one elsewhere on the homepage.

## 7. Capabilities

**What you see:** "Scanning capabilities", the heading "3D scanning services for every geometry and
application", an intro about the Costa Mesa lab, and six photos with a caption under each.

**Built from:**

- A **Group** set to **No lines, stacked on tablet**: the head (**Eyebrow**, **Heading large**) on the left,
  the **Lede** on the right.
- A **Group** set to **Grid with lines** (3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on phones). Each photo is a
  **Group** with **Lines between blocks** holding an **Image** (Tangent style **Media**, 4:3) and a caption
  (**Text**, **Eyebrow, leading mark**).

**Safe to change:** the photos and captions. Keep six photos (two full rows) and the same 4:3 shape for all.
Give each photo alt text.

## 8. Scanning options

**What you see:** three columns: In-house scanning, Shop scanning, Mobile, large-scale scanning. Each has an
icon, a short text and three check chips, such as "Same-day available".

**Built from:** a **Group** set to **Grid with lines** (3 columns on desktop, 1 on tablet and phones). Each
column is a **Group** with an **Icon** (**Accent**) and a group with a **Card title**, a **Body small** text
and a **Text** in **Chips, with checks**. The chips are a bulleted list in that text block: one bullet, one
chip.

**Safe to change:** the words and the chips. Keep two to four short chips per column.

## 9. Call to action

**What you see:** "Tell us about your project", a line about quoting in one business day, and the phone and
**Send a message** buttons, with a faint dot pattern at the end.

**Built from:** the section's **Background pattern** is **Dot lattice at the end**. Inside, a **Group** set
to **Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet** holds three groups: the **Heading medium**, the **Lede** and
the two **Button** blocks.

**Safe to change:** the words and the buttons. The phone button links to `tel:` plus the number; the message
button links to `/pages/contact-us`.

## Common changes

- **Change a text:** click it in the preview, edit it, click **Save**. See
  [Editing content](/editing-pages/editing-content).
- **Change the phone number:** the two call buttons on the homepage carry the number in their **Label** and
  **Link**. See [Change the phone number](/how-to/change-phone-number).
- **Replace a photo or logo:** see [Replace an image](/how-to/replace-image).
- **Change a service cell or a reason:** open its **Group** and edit the **Text** and **Icon** inside. See
  [Icons](/design-rules/icons) before you change an icon.
- **Hide a section for a while:** use the eye icon next to its name instead of deleting it. See
  [Editing content](/editing-pages/editing-content).
