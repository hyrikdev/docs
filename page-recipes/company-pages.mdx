The company pages tell visitors who Tangent is and what it does. Each has a template of its own, named after the
page: **about-us**, **our-company**, **team**, **portfolio**, **3d-scan** and **services**. Plain text pages,
such as /pages/thank-you, use the **Default page** template. Almost everything on these pages is typed in the
theme editor; the exceptions are listed per page.

| Page | Address | Template |
|---|---|---|
| About us | /pages/about-us | **about-us** |
| Our company | /pages/our-company | **our-company** |
| Meet our team | /pages/team | **team** |
| Portfolio | /pages/portfolio | **portfolio** |
| Reverse engineering scanner | /pages/3d-scan | **3d-scan** |
| Services overview | /pages/services | **services** |
| Plain text pages | for example /pages/thank-you | **Default page** |

## The closing call on every company page

Every company page ends with a **Call to action** section: "Tell us about your project", a one-line text, the
phone button and **Send a message** (a link to the contact page). It looks the same everywhere, but each template
has its own copy. If you change its words, change them on each page. The services overview has its own words
in this section (see below).

## Plain text pages (Default page)

Pages that only need a title and some text use the **Default page** template, for example /pages/thank-you. It
has two sections:

| Section | What you see | Where the words come from |
|---|---|---|
| **Page** | Breadcrumbs, the title, an optional intro, then the page text. | The title is the page's headline field, or else the page **Title**. The intro is the hero subheading field. The text is the page **Content**. All are in the page record in the admin. |
| **Call to action** | The closing call. | Typed in the theme editor. This copy is shared by every page on the **Default page** template. |

To make a new plain page, add the page in **Online Store → Pages**, write its **Content** and leave
**Theme template** on **Default page**. See [Pages and SEO](/admin/pages-and-seo).

<Warning>
The **Default page** template is shared. A change to its sections changes every plain text page at once.
</Warning>

## What's safe to change

- **Words, photos and buttons** in any typed block. Keep the [text style](/blocks/text-styles) each block
  already has.
- **Cards** in a card list: add, remove or reorder them. Keep the icon rules in
  [Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont).
- **Don't** change a section's **Tangent frame** settings or a group's **Lines**: the column lines line up from
  one section to the next.
- **Don't** switch one of these pages to another template. The words live in the template, so the page would
  show another page's content.
