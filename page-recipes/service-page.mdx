Each service page, such as Reverse engineering or 3D inspection, has its own template named **service-**
plus the page's address. There are 13 of them. A service page explains one service and ends on the quote
form. Almost all of its words and photos are typed in the theme editor, in that page's template.

## What every service page has

Every service template starts with **Service hero** and ends with **Quote**. Everything in between varies.

### Service hero (top)

**What you see:** the breadcrumbs Home / Services / page, the page title, often an intro, the **Call us
now** and **Get a Quote** buttons, and a photo on the right. On some pages a row of key figures sits under
it, for example "0.025mm".

**Built from:**

- **Breadcrumbs**, Tangent style **Strip: own ruled row**, with **Parent label** "Services" and
  **Parent link** `/pages/services`. The last step is the page's **Title** from the admin.
  See [Breadcrumbs](/blocks/breadcrumbs).
- A **Group** with **Lines between blocks**, holding:
  - a **Group** set to **Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**: on the left a group with the eyebrow
    (on some pages), the title (**Text**, **Heading large**), the intro (**Lede**) and a group with two
    **Button** blocks; on the right a group with the photo (**Image**, Tangent style **Media**);
  - on some pages, a **Card list** of key figures (layout **Stats** or **Drawing notes**, 4 or 2 columns).

**Where the words come from:** typed in the theme editor, except the last breadcrumb, which is the page
title in the admin.

**Safe to change:** the words, the photo and the key figures. **Call us now** links to `tel:` plus the phone
number. **Get a Quote** links to `#quote`, which jumps to the form at the bottom: keep that link.

### Quote (bottom)

**What you see:** a heading such as "Tell us about your project", sometimes a short line under it, the phone,
email, address and hours, and the quote form on the right.

**Built from:** a **Group** set to **Lines between blocks, stacked on tablet**. The left group holds a
**Heading large**, sometimes a **Lede**, and a **Contact details** block. The right group holds the
**Quote form** block (its **Form name (for tracking)** is "service"). See
[Contact details and quote form](/blocks/contact-and-quote-form).

**Where the words come from:** typed in the theme editor. The **Contact details** block has the phone
number typed in its **Phone** field on every service page.

**Safe to change:** the heading and the line under it. Leave the form's **Anchor ID** as `quote`, or the
**Get a Quote** buttons stop jumping to it.

## Sections that only some pages have

The other service pages mix the sections above with these. The name in the sidebar tells you what each one is.

| **Closing line** | One sentence in **Closing line** style | 3D mobile laser scanning, Mobile laser scanning, Model rendering |

## Where the words come from

| What | Where to change it |
|---|---|
| Headings, texts, cards, photos, buttons | Theme editor, in the page's own **service-** template |
| The last breadcrumb | The page's **Title** in **Online Store → Pages** |
| FAQ questions and answers | **FAQ** entries in the admin. The page's **FAQ category** block picks which category it shows. See [FAQ](/blocks/faq) |
| Case study cards | The **Case studies** blog in **Online Store → Blog posts**. The newest posts show by themselves. See [Blog posts](/admin/blog-posts) |
| Search title and description | The page's **Search engine listing** in the admin. See [Pages and SEO](/admin/pages-and-seo) |

## What's safe to change

- **Words, photos, icons and card order** in any section: yes. Click the block in the preview or the
  sidebar, edit, **Save**.
- **Hiding a section** you don't need for a while: yes, with the eye icon.
- **Adding a section:** copy one from another service page so its lines and spacing match. Don't place two
  card grids of the same kind back to back. See [Do's and don'ts](/design-rules/do-and-dont#layout).
- **Card list settings** (layout, columns, icon style): only to match another page. Industry, benefit and
  reason cards always keep **Bare icon, 24px**.
- **Breadcrumbs, the form's Anchor ID and Form name:** leave them as they are.
