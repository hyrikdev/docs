The HandySCAN Black Elite scanner has a product page at /products/handyscan-black-elite. It uses the template
**quote**. The site doesn't sell online, so the page has no price and no cart: visitors call or book a call
instead. The theme also has Shopify's default product template; the scanner doesn't use it.

## Product record or theme editor?

A **product** in Shopify is a record in **Products** in the admin, with its own title, description and photos.
The template decides what's shown around them.

| Edit in the product record (**Products** → HandySCAN Black Elite) | Edit in the theme editor (template **quote**) |
|---|---|
| **Title**: the big heading and the last step of the breadcrumbs. | The Creaform and Tangent logos above the title. |
| **Description**: the text under the title. | The **Call Now** and **Schedule a call** buttons. |
| **Media**: the photos on the left. | Every section below the top: applications, photos, benefits, specs, software, portability and the closing call. |
| **Search engine listing**: the title and description in Google. | |
| **Theme template**: keep it on **quote**. | |

<Note>
The specs, software and portability texts used to be fields of the product record. They're now typed in the
theme editor, like the rest of the sections below the top. The product record no longer holds them.
</Note>

## The brochure

The "Download the full brochure here." link is the last line of the **Specs** text. It points to the brochure
PDF in **Content → Files**. To replace the brochure:

<Frame caption="The Specs section: the brochure link is the last line of the text">
  <img src="/images/product-specs.png" alt="The heading 'Precise, reliable, and high-resolution 3D scanning from the HandySCAN Black Elite', the spec text with accuracy lists and the link 'Download the full brochure here.' on the left, two photos of the scanner in use on the right" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the new PDF">
    In the admin, go to **Content → Files** and upload the new brochure. Copy its link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the link">
    In the theme editor, open the **quote** template, click the **Text** block in the **Specs** section, select
    the link text and paste the new link with the link button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check">
    Click **Save**, then open the page and click the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's safe to change

- **Title, description and photos** in the product record. Keep the description short: it sits beside the photos.
- **Texts, cards and photos** of the sections below, in the theme editor.
- **Don't** set the product's template to the default product template: the page would lose every section
  below the top and show Shopify's standard product layout.
- **Don't** add price, quantity or **Add to cart** blocks. The site takes requests by phone, booking or the
  contact form.
