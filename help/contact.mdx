When you ask for help, include:

- the address of the page, for example `usetangent.com/pages/chicago`;
- what you expected and what you see, with a screenshot of the desktop or the phone view;
- which theme you were editing (the current theme or a copy);
- what you changed just before the problem started.

## Ask your developer for

- changing the phone number, address or email on every page at once;
- new page layouts or new kinds of sections;
- several new location or service pages at once;
- restoring an older version of a page layout;
- fixing old `/services/` links, which needs a change outside Shopify.

## Shopify help

For questions about the Shopify admin itself (accounts, billing, settings), use the Shopify Help Center at
[help.shopify.com](https://help.shopify.com) or the **Help** button in the admin.
