## What a theme is

A **theme** is the design of a Shopify site: the layout of every kind of page, the fonts, the colors, the
header and the footer. It's like a WordPress theme together with the page builder layouts.

What a theme is **not**: your content records. Pages, blog posts, menus, images and FAQ entries live in the
Shopify admin and stay put when you change or copy a theme.

A store can hold several themes. One is live (Shopify calls it the **current theme**); the others wait in the
**Theme library**, where only staff can see them. See [Preview and publish](/getting-started/preview-and-publish).

## Horizon, Shopify's own theme

**Horizon** is a theme made by Shopify itself. The Tangent site is built on it.

Horizon is built from **blocks that hold other blocks**. A section can hold groups, a group can hold more groups,
and so on down to a single text or button. That's what lets you rearrange a page in the theme editor without
code: you drag blocks up and down, in and out of groups, the way you moved widgets in Elementor. See
[Sections, groups and blocks](/horizon/sections-groups-blocks) for how this looks on a real page.

## The Tangent layer

On its own, Horizon looks like a shop. The Tangent design (the ruled grid, the blue headings, the thin lines, the
line icons) comes from additions made for this site. They live inside the theme, so you see them as ordinary
settings and blocks in the theme editor.

### 1. Tangent settings on Horizon's own blocks

Several of Horizon's blocks got extra settings, grouped under a name that starts with **Tangent**:

| Block | Tangent settings | What they do | More |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Text** | **Tangent style** | Turns a text into an eyebrow, a heading, an intro, chips, ticks and so on. | [Text styles](/blocks/text-styles) |
| **Button** | **Tangent style** | The square Tangent buttons (**Primary, neutral**, **Secondary**, **Accent**), with the phone icon on call buttons. | [Buttons](/blocks/buttons) |
| **Image** | **Tangent style** | How the picture is framed: **Media** for photos, **Logo** or **Logo in a row** for client logos. | [Images](/blocks/images) |
| **Section** | **Tangent frame** | The rails down both sides of the page, the line between sections, the faint dot pattern. | [Section layout](/blocks/section-layout) |
| **Group** | **Tangent grid** | Lines between cells, ruled grids, and how many columns on each screen size. | [Section layout](/blocks/section-layout#group-settings) |

### 3. One shared set of design values

The Tangent colors, fonts, spacing and corner shapes are written down once, in a single list in the theme files.
Developers call these values **design tokens**. Every Tangent block reads from that list:

- **Colors**: near-black text on white, and one accent blue (#0562EF) for headings, icons and selected items.
- **Fonts**: Geist for headings, Inter for text, Geist Mono for small labels. They come with the theme.
- **Shapes**: square corners and thin 1-pixel lines.

That's why every page looks alike without you setting colors or fonts, and why the color and font settings in
**Theme settings** don't restyle the Tangent blocks. See [Theme settings](/horizon/theme-settings).

### 4. Site-wide behavior

A few things happen by themselves on every page:

- **No shopping cart.** The site sells by quote and phone. A Tangent setting hides the cart and stops checkout,
  even on the scanner's product page.
- **Links to other websites** open in a new tab and get a diagonal arrow (↗).
- **Sections with nothing to show hide themselves** when **Hide section when empty** is on. See
  [Hide section when empty](/horizon/sections-groups-blocks#hide-section-when-empty).
