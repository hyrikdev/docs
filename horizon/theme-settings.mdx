**Theme settings** are the settings that apply to the whole site at once, not to one page: the logo, the
favicon, colors, fonts, buttons and so on. They come in groups; each group has its own page in this guide.

## Open them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize**. See [Open the theme editor](/getting-started/theme-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to theme settings">
    In the narrow bar on the far left of the editor, click the **Theme settings** icon. The sidebar now lists
    setting groups instead of the page's sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a group">
    Click a group, for example **Logo and favicon**, to see its settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A theme setting changes **every page** at once. On the live theme, **Save** puts it live straight away. Try any
change on a copy of the theme first: see [Preview and publish](/getting-started/preview-and-publish).
</Warning>

## Why most of these don't change the look

The Tangent blocks get their colors, fonts, buttons and spacing from the Tangent design, not from these
settings (see [What is Horizon](/horizon/what-is-horizon#3-one-shared-set-of-design-values)). Every text and
button on the site's pages uses a **Tangent style**, so changing a font or a button color here doesn't restyle
them. It only reaches the few parts Horizon still draws on its own, which then no longer match the rest.

A change here goes one of 3 ways:

- **Nothing visible happens**, because the Tangent design paints over it: for example **Buttons** or the
  **Typography** presets.
- **The design breaks**, because the Tangent design is built on it: for example **Page width** or the page
  **Background**.
- **You get what you meant**: only the logo and the favicon work that way.

So the rule is simple: **change the logo and the favicon if the brand changes, and leave everything else as it
is.**

<Note>
For developers: the Tangent design values live in the theme's token files, and the Tangent styles are settings on
the blocks themselves. See [Design system](/technical/design-system).
</Note>

## The groups

In the order the editor lists them:

| Group | What it changes | On this site | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Logo and favicon** | The logo in the header and footer, the header logo's height, the browser-tab icon. | Safe to change, with care | [Logo and favicon](/theme-settings/logo-and-favicon) |
| **Color palette** | The 4 base colors other settings link to. Its background shows behind every page. | Leave as is | [Color palette](/theme-settings/color-palette) |
| **Typography** | Horizon's fonts and text presets. Tangent text styles ignore them. | Leave as is | [Typography](/theme-settings/typography) |
| **Page** | The page background and the page width (the rails). | Leave as is | [Page](/theme-settings/page) |
| **Animations** | Page transition and card effects, all off. | Leave as is | [Animations](/theme-settings/animations) |
| **Badges** | Sale and sold-out labels on product cards. Not on the site. | Leave as is | [Badges](/theme-settings/badges) |
| **Buttons** | Horizon's own button looks. Tangent buttons ignore them. | Leave as is | [Buttons](/theme-settings/buttons) |
| **Cart** | The cart page and drawer, and **Block cart and checkout**, which makes the site quote-only. | Leave as is: keep **Block cart and checkout** on | [Store features](/theme-settings/store-features#cart), [Block cart and checkout](/theme-settings/tangent) |
| **Drawers** | Horizon's slide-out panels. The phone menu ignores them. | Leave as is | [Drawers](/theme-settings/drawers) |
| **Icons** | The line weight of Horizon's small icons: header phone, menu button, FAQ plus signs. | Leave as is | [Icons](/theme-settings/icons) |
| **Input fields** | Horizon's form fields. The quote form ignores them. | Leave as is | [Input fields](/theme-settings/input-fields) |
| **Popovers and modals** | Horizon's small pop-up panels. None show on the site. | Leave as is | [Popovers and modals](/theme-settings/popovers-and-modals) |
| **Prices** | Currency codes after prices. No prices on the site. | Leave as is | [Store features](/theme-settings/store-features#prices) |
| **Product cards** | Quick add and photos of product tiles. Not on the site. | Leave as is | [Store features](/theme-settings/store-features#product-cards) |
| **Search** | The search panel of the header. The header has no search. | Leave as is | [Store features](/theme-settings/store-features#search) |
| **Swatches** | Color swatches for product options. Not on the site. | Leave as is | [Store features](/theme-settings/store-features#swatches) |
| **Variant pickers** | Option buttons of product pages. Not on the site. | Leave as is | [Store features](/theme-settings/store-features#variant-pickers) |

## If you changed something by mistake

Use **undo** in the top bar before you save. After saving, set the value back by hand using the **Tangent uses**
column on the group's page, or ask your developer: the theme files hold the last approved values. See
[Undo and versions](/getting-started/undo-and-versions).
