---
title: "Do's and don'ts"
description: "Simple rules that keep every page looking like one site."
---

The Tangent design is calm and precise, like a technical drawing: thin lines, plenty of white space, one
accent color. These rules keep it that way when you edit.

## Icons

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Give every card in a grid of industries, applications, benefits, reasons or options a line icon.
    Use **Bare icon, 24px** on the card list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Put icons in boxes or colored tiles. Mix icon sizes. Use two different icons for the same idea.
  </Card>
</Columns>

- **Same idea, same icon.** Precision is always **Tangent target**, mobile scanning is always **Tangent truck**,
  as on the homepage. See the [icon table](/design-rules/icons).
- **Cards with a photo get no icon.** The photo is already the marker.
- **One icon size.** Don't resize icons or switch a list to a different icon style.
- **No icons** on deliverables, comparisons, capabilities, photo grids, quotes or logos.

## Text

- **Sentence case everywhere.** Capitalize only the first word and names: "3D scanning services across
  demanding industries", not "3D Scanning Services Across Demanding Industries", and never ALL CAPS.
  The menu items and the "Call Now" / "Get a Quote" buttons keep their current capitals.
- **Card titles stay black.** Section headings are blue; card titles aren't.
- **One main heading per page.** Only the page title uses **Display (hero title)**.
- **Use Tangent's own words.** Don't add filler text, made-up quotes or stock photos to fill a gap. An empty cell
  is better than invented content.

## Phones

  <Frame caption="Do: on phones, all headings start on the same left edge">
    <img src="/images/headings-phone.png" alt="A section on a phone: eyebrow, heading, intro and cards all start at the same left edge" />
  </Frame>
  <Frame caption="Do: on phones, each text is followed by its own image">
    <img src="/images/card-list-pairs-phone.png" alt="On a phone, each text card is followed by its matching image" />
  </Frame>
</Columns>

- **Headings line up on the left on phones.** Even a heading that's centered on desktop starts at the same left
  edge as the text below it on a phone. The theme does this for you: don't add spaces or padding to "center"
  a heading.
- **Each text is followed by its image.** When texts and photos come in pairs, phones show text 1, image 1,
  text 2, image 2. Use **Text cards over their images** on the card list. See
  [Card list](/blocks/card-list#text-then-its-image-on-phones).
- **Check every change in the mobile view** before you save.

## Links and buttons

- **Links to other websites open in a new tab** and show a diagonal arrow (↗). This happens by itself.
  Links to Tangent pages open in the same tab with a straight arrow (→).
- **Phone first.** The call button comes first and is black; the contact or quote button comes second and is
  white.
- **Every call button shows the phone icon.**
- **Link to Tangent pages with their short address**, for example `/pages/reverse-engineering`.

## Layout

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Do" icon="check">
    Keep the thin lines, the white space and the square corners. Copy a section from another page when you need
    a new one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't" icon="x">
    Add boxes, shadows, rounded "pill" buttons, colored backgrounds or extra borders around content.
  </Card>
</Columns>

- **Blue is the accent.** It's for headings, icons, selected items and progress. Don't use blue buttons or blue
  backgrounds for ordinary content.
- **Two-column rows split in half**, and column lines line up from one section to the next.
- **Decorations are rare.** The faint pixel squares and dot patterns appear at most once per page, in empty
  space, never over text.
- **Don't place the same kind of block twice in a row** (for example two identical card grids back to back).
  Vary the rhythm: a grid, then a text and photo row, then a grid.

## Images

- Use Tangent's own project photos, at least 1600 pixels wide for wide frames.
- All photos in one row share the same shape.
- Screenshots, reports and drawings with text are shown whole (**Show the whole image**), never cropped.
- Every image has alt text. See [Files and images](/admin/files-and-images).
